Leaders, VP and above, who run engineering, platform, product, data or AI teams at India's growth and late-stage tech companies.

Yes. CTOs and founder-CTOs are at the core of the room, alongside the engineering, product, data and AI leaders who work with them.

Yes, if you lead at VP level or above. Engineering leaders are the largest group in the room.

Yes. Product leaders are part of the room by design. The name says CTO; the room is five functions wide.
