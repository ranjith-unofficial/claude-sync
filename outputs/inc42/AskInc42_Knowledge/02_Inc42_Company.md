## 1. What Inc42 is

- **India's startup media and intelligence platform** — news, in-depth reporting, startup data, research and events covering India's startup economy.
- **Founded January 2014.**
- Reporting is picked up by the **Wall Street Journal, Bloomberg, Reuters and TechCrunch**.

**Scale:**

| Stories published | 50,000+ |
