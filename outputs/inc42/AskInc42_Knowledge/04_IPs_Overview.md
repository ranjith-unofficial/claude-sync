# Inc42 IPs — Overview

**Source S3 · v2 · Verified against live sites 14 Aug 2026 · Owner: Ashish · Review: WEEKLY**

The eleven approved Inc42 event and programme IPs. High level by design — enough to answer "what is it, who is it for, when is it, am I eligible, where do I go", and nothing more.

## Never disclose

- **Any price, fee, pass tier, discount, promotional offer or seat availability.** For every pricing question: redirect to that IP's website.
- **Any refund or cancellation term.** One answer only: Contact Us. See `06_Response_Rules.md`.
- Sponsor commercials, partnership terms or delegate targets.
- Any IP not listed in this document.

## Three rules that apply to every entry

1. **State the status first.** Several editions below have already concluded. The dates are on every record — answer from them. Never describe a concluded edition as if it's upcoming.
2. **Access model may be stated; money sends them to the website.** Whether an IP is free, paid or invite-only varies by event and is stable, so say it. The moment a question moves to *how much*, redirect to that IP's site — no amounts, ever.
3. **Eligibility is answerable.** Say who an IP is for and what the published criteria are. Where a criterion is a threshold figure, describe it qualitatively and point to the programme page for the exact number. **Never rule on whether a specific user qualifies.**

**Use the URL exactly as written.** Some IPs live on `events.inc42.com`, some on their own domains. A wrong host is a dead link.

## 6. D2CX Runway

- **Full name:** D2CX Runway, by Inc42 and **Rukam Capital**.
- **Status:** **the 2026 cohort has concluded** — Demo Day was 7 August 2026. The next cohort has not been announced.
- **What it is:** a high-intensity hybrid accelerator for D2C brands entering their next growth phase — a virtual bootcamp, then a physical residency in Delhi NCR, ending in a Demo Day. Built around three pillars: brand building, operational excellence and capital readiness. Selected brands access a dedicated investment pool.
- **Cohort size:** 15 startups, shortlisted through a deeper evaluation. The inaugural cohort had 14 brands across fashion, F&B, babycare, jewellery and automotive.
- **Who it's for:** D2C and consumer-tech brands — beauty and personal care, F&B, fashion, health and similar — in the early growth stage, bootstrapped through pre-Series A, with a live product, consistent month-on-month growth and full-time founders able to attend the residency. Criteria are on the programme page.
- ⚠️ **Do not state a programme duration.** The page is titled a "4-week accelerator" while the body describes a longer virtual bootcamp plus a residency, and press coverage has said both four and seven weeks. Describe the structure instead: virtual bootcamp → physical residency → Demo Day.
- **Website:** `inc42.com/d2cx-runway`

## 11. D2CX Foundations

- **Full name:** D2CX Foundations by Inc42.
- **What it is:** a 6-week programme for launching a D2C brand — how to build the foundations of a profitable one. Pre-recorded masterclasses plus weekly assignments and regular live Q&A sessions, with content unlocking weekly.
- **Who it's for:** first-time and early D2C founders, retail brand owners moving into digital, and operators launching their first D2C brand. This is the **pre-revenue / just-starting** counterpart to D2CX.
- **Eligibility:** **no application or interview** — direct enrolment.
- **What participants get:** expert-led sessions across growth, branding, marketing and operations · mentorship · a growth roadmap · access to a founder-only investor network · lifetime alumni community access · vendor deals · an optional in-person convocation in Delhi NCR.
- **Access:** **paid programme.** Redirect to the site for anything commercial.
- **Website:** `foundations.d2cx.co`
