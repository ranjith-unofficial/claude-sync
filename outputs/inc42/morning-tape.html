7 items · ranked by cost
Android app errors nearly tripled — 1 in 9 active users hit one
Bug
28 error_shown events across 8 of 69 daily active users, against a 10/day baseline. 27 of 28 were Android on v1.0.0 — load_failed on the brief (15) and Explore (10), plus 2 on company profile. iOS, now on 1.0.1, threw exactly one. Android has never received a 1.0.1 build, so the fix that appears to have helped iOS has not shipped there.
A referral source that was sending up to 1,400 visitors/day went to zero
Traffic
news.squidapp.co sent 448, 439 and 1,432 visitors on 13–15 Aug, then 10 on 18 Aug and nothing on 19 or 20 Aug. That gap alone accounts for most of yesterday's 13% visitor decline. Nobody appears to have noticed it stop — worth finding out whether it was their change or ours.
Pro payments are double-counted across two PostHog projects
Data trust
Payment Completed_Backend lands in both the web project (53557) and DataLabs (66351), with byte-identical daily counts every day for the last eight. Adding the two projects' "Pro Payment" totals doubles revenue. Yesterday's real number is 6 buyers, not 37 + 7 = 44 events.
The frontend payment-success event misses two thirds of buyers
Data trust
Yesterday: Payment Completed fired for 2 users, Payment Completed_Backend for 6. The pattern holds all week (13 vs 3 on 18 Aug). Anything measuring conversion off the frontend event is understating it. Until it's fixed, treat the backend stage as the only source of truth.
Newsletter signups fell to 12 — lowest in 15 days
Watch
Against a 39/day average and a 15-day range of 17–52. Registrations (59) and logins (114) were both normal-to-good on the same day, so this is specific to the newsletter path rather than general traffic weakness. One day only — confirm today before acting.
DataLabs Limit Reached has fired zero times in 15 days
Hygiene
The event exists in the taxonomy and never fires. Either the usage limit it tracks is unreachable, or the instrumentation is broken. Meanwhile Register Lock Interaction fires ~150×/day, so gating itself is clearly working — it's this one event that's dark.
Internal users are still not excluded from the web and app projects
Hygiene
DataLabs filters test accounts by default; projects 53557 (web) and 146258 (app) do not. At 69 app DAU, the team's own usage is a material share of every app number on this page. Until that filter exists, read app figures as an upper bound.

How to read this

Method & caveats

Comparison basis. Every "change" column is yesterday against the mean of the seven days before it (13–19 Aug), not against the day before. Day-over-day on this data is mostly weekend noise — both Sat 15 and Sun 16 Aug run 30–40% below weekdays on all three properties.

Days are IST. The app project stores in UTC, so its days are converted; web and DataLabs are already Asia/Kolkata. Today (21 Aug) is excluded everywhere — it is partial.

Unique users are counted on person_id, not distinct_id, so one person across devices counts once. Uniques don't sum across days, which is why weekly totals are deliberately absent from this page.

Three numbers on this page should not be trusted yet, and they're flagged in the attention list: Pro payments are duplicated across two projects, the frontend payment-success event under-fires, and internal traffic is not filtered out of the web or app projects.
