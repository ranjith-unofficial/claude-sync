Panel · 30 min

The Architecture Of 10X: Engineering For India's Next Scale Curve

Vikas Goyal Cofounder & CTO, KukuIsh Babbar Cofounder & CTO, InsuranceDekhoVaibhav Khandelwal Cofounder & CTO, Shadowfax

5 talking points
  1. Engineering for Indian peaks: festive sales, 9:15 AM trading, IPL traffic, UPI spikes, and the moments when carefully designed capacity assumptions collapse
  2. Architecture lessons from scaling across patchy networks, low-end devices, tier-2/3 markets and millions of concurrent users
  3. Using AI for demand forecasting, capacity planning, anomaly detection and incident response, with real examples of where it prevented failure and where it added complexity
  4. The Indian playbook for graceful degradation: what companies deliberately slow, switch off or simplify when keeping everything live is no longer possible
  5. Architecture decisions scaled Indian companies are now reversing as AI workloads, inference traffic and the next 10X hit stacks built in a very different era
10:30–11:00

Panel · 30 min

Building AI Systems For Indian Scale

Nitin Jain CTO, ShareChat & Moj

6 talking points
  1. From demo to daily traffic: what changes in architecture and cost when an AI feature goes from an impressive pilot to tens of millions of Indian users
  2. The inference bill as a design constraint: cost per query and per completed action; model routing (frontier vs distilled vs on-device), caching and batching, and who decides when to swap models
  3. Latency on Indian networks and devices: patchy 4G, low-end Androids, voice-first users; what runs on-device, what round-trips, what gets precomputed
  4. Hinglish, vernacular and noisy input at scale: input pipelines and evals built around real Indian customer journeys rather than generic global benchmarks
  5. When the model misbehaves in production: drift, vendor updates, regressions and cost spikes; the monitoring and fallback architecture designed before launch
  6. Build vs rent for the AI serving layer: gateways, eval infra, routing; what a scaled Indian company must own and what it should never build

Panel · 30 min

Why Verification Is Becoming The New Engineering Bottleneck

Speakers announcing soon

5 talking points
  1. What changes when AI coding moves from a few developers experimenting to 30%, 50% or more of production code being AI-assisted
  2. The new bottleneck: code is generated faster, while senior review, testing, security and architecture capacity struggle to keep pace
  3. Measuring the real productivity gain through release frequency, rework, defects, rollback rates and time-to-production rather than lines of code or PR counts
  4. Production incidents and near misses caused by AI-generated code, and the verification, approval or testing processes companies changed afterwards
  5. The skills verification now demands: debugging, architecture judgement, system context and recognising when the machine is confidently wrong
12:00–12:30

Panel · 30 min

The Great Engineering Reset: Teams, Talent & Leadership After AI

Kausal Malladi CTO, Investments, INDmoney

6 talking points
  1. The AI-era org chart: which roles are shrinking, growing or being redesigned as AI absorbs routine coding, QA and coordination
  2. The smaller-team question: where lean creates speed, and where it quietly creates fragility through on-call pressure, key-person risk and lost institutional knowledge
  3. The missing middle: junior output rises while the mid-senior layer that mentors, reviews and owns architecture thins out; who trains the next generation
  4. Hiring in 2026: what the fresher bar now tests when every candidate has AI in the room
  5. The new career ladder: what differentiates senior, staff and EM when writing code gets easier, and which skills compound
  6. The 2027 talent mix: where CTOs would add headcount, where they'd stay lean, and which human capabilities they'd protect

Panel · 30 min

Building The Data Foundation For AI At Scale

Anil Sinha CTO, FibeThiyagaraj T Director, Engineering, Eightfold

6 talking points
  1. The moment companies discover that data stacks built for dashboards and reporting are not clean, fast or contextual enough for production AI
  2. Real data problems derailing AI projects: fragmented customer IDs, messy catalogues, duplicate records, stale inventory, inconsistent payments data and unclear ownership
  3. Where real-time data genuinely matters across payments, commerce, mobility, logistics and fraud, versus expensive pipelines with no measurable business impact
  4. Data-access design for the AI era: which datasets models and agents may read, who owns them, and the lineage and context that make outputs dependable
  5. Multi-tenant AI: keeping per-customer data isolated when models and agents want context across everything
  6. The first fixes CTOs can make to become AI-ready: cleaning critical datasets, assigning ownership, fixing lineage and prioritising a few high-value data flows

FAQ

Before you book.

I lead product, data or AI, not engineering. Is this for me?

Yes. Product leaders are 15% of the room and data and AI leaders another 15%. The name says CTO. The room is five functions wide.

What happens if my application isn't approved?

You get a full refund within 7 working days. If you're a fit for the room but applied under the wrong category, our team will contact you and guide you on next steps.

What exactly does the pass include?

Access to every session, Q&A with speakers, executive lunch, high tea, dinner and networking, and 634 delegate deals worth $320K+ in credits. There's no separate registration for individual sessions.

Is the $320K+ in credits real money off my bills?

They're provider credits and free plans, not cash. The cloud, AI and data credits listed on this page add up to $420,750 at each provider's lower tier and about $1.25M at higher tiers, which usually apply to funded companies. Each provider sets its own eligibility and terms.

Is this a CIO event?

No. CIO conferences cover governance, digital transformation and IT strategy. We don't. If you own a platform, a product, a data function or an AI roadmap, this is your room.

Is the agenda final?

The morning panels from 10:00 to 13:30 are confirmed. Two 25-minute firesides and the afternoon sessions from 15:00 to 18:00 are being finalised and will be published on this page.

Can I transfer my pass to a colleague?

No. Once confirmed, a pass can't be shared or transferred to another person.

Can my company's GST number go on the booking?

Yes. Add your GST number, country and state at checkout.

I want to speak or sponsor.

To sponsor, fill in the sponsorship form. For speaking opportunities, contact the team and choose "Speakership Opportunity".
