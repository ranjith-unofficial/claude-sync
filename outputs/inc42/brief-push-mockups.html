How slot 1 gets filled, and why depth stops going missing

Three asks — breaking news on top, in-depth always covered, notification aligned to both — resolve into one change: give the brief a fixed slot structure instead of a ranked list truncated at 8.

SlotHoldsChosen byIf unavailable
1 The lead Editorial pin, one per brief window. This is where breaking news goes. Falls back to highest-scoring News-topic article by recency. Never empty.
2 or 3 Reserved in-depth Most recent in-depth in the window, regardless of what time it published. Slot releases back to ranked news. Happened once in 15 days.
rest Ranked news Existing locked formula, personalised. Unchanged.

Depth lands at slot 2 or 3 rather than at the end because drop-off is concentrated at the start of the brief. A reserved slot at position 8 would be reserved for people who already finished. The exact position is tunable; the reservation is the point.

This fixes the problem without touching editorial's clock. Editorial confirmed there is no pattern behind publishing times, and the data agrees — 11 of 12 evening pieces went live at ragged minutes. A reserved slot makes the brief indifferent to when a piece published, which is the only version of this that survives an unmanaged publishing schedule.

SituationHow oftenBehaviour

Sunday recap, 10 from ~85weekly Reserve 2 to 3 depth slots — the weekly pool holds roughly 8 in-depth pieces
Monday weekend brief, 9 from ~11weekly Thin pool. Depth is usually there but only from Sat evening or Sun morning
Two things have to be settled before this can be built. First, there is no "breaking" signal in the ranking formula today. The cheapest route is to constrain App Featured = Yes to one article per brief window and treat it as the lead; the cleaner route is a separate lead flag. Before reusing App Featured, confirm what else it drives. Second, this only guarantees alignment if the brief is materialised server-side at 07:00. If it is computed on open, that has to move first.

All three are identical, and that is the ranking formula working as designed. Editorial "App Featured = Yes" is pinned to the top of every brief regardless of what the user follows. On any day with an App Featured article, approach D and approach B produce the same notification — for every user.
