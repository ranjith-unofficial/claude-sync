## Wispr summary
Working session on unifying Inc42 across Media, Data Labs, IP, and App: aligned on onboarding, navigation, and login content deliverables, but activation metric (QIA) definition stayed unresolved pending more thought.

### One Inc42 Vision & Unified Experience
- Goal: unified UI, search, and messaging so users don't perceive Media, Data Labs, IP, App as separate sites
- Same top nav across products; sign-up flows to one onboarding regardless of source (e.g. events sub-sites)
- App login should support phone number or email for new users; existing users lack phone login

### Onboarding & Enrichment
- Collect only 3 fields: job title, company name, plus interest/sector; rest enriched real-time from job title and company
- Interest/sector must be asked since it cannot be derived from enrichment

### Acquisition & Sign-up Incentives
- Article pages are the main conversion lever; incentivize sign-up via free sector-specific PDF reports from Data Labs
- Login modal copy must differ by source: Media vs Data Labs entry points need different value props

### QIA Metric Debate (Unresolved)
- QIA = Qualified (seniority) + Identified (signed up, onboarded) + Activated (any of 10 actions in 7 days)
- Speaker 1 pushed for single unified activation definition across Inc42; Speaker 2 argued products differ so activation must be per-source
    - Speaker 1 concern: 10 flows in CIO per activation path is unmanageable
    - Data shows 70%+ of signups activate within 7 days; 96% on app
- Deferred: both to think more and revisit Wednesday

### Cross-workspace Data & CIO
- Unified workspace needed so web (PostHog) and app activity talk to each other for cross-pollination nudges
- Open question: how to migrate old data without loss like last transfer; Speaker 2 evaluating approaches

### Next Steps
- (Speaker 1) Deliver content this week: top nav items + order, subsections, Media home login, Data Labs modal copy, article lock modal copy
- (Speaker 1) Define post-7-day segmentation buckets (champion, super, active, dormant, lapsed) with Animesh and Amit
- (Speaker 1) Rethink QIA/activation definition before Wednesday follow-up
- (Speaker 1) Add interest/sector field to onboarding spec
- (Speaker 2) Share Utkarsh's document and own document on metrics
- (Speaker 2) Lock designs next week once content is received; build dedicated app landing page this month
- (Speaker 2) Figure out unified-workspace approach that preserves historical data
- (Speaker 2) Give Speaker 1 CIO access to edit button copy for experimentation

### Decisions Made
- Onboarding collects only job title, company name, and interest/sector; other attributes enriched from job title and company
- Activation window stays at 7 days, not 15
- Activation requires any one of the 10 defined actions, not all
- Dedicated app landing page will ship, but not in the initial release
- Newsletter-only sign-up dropped as acquisition channel; incentivize with tangible benefits instead
