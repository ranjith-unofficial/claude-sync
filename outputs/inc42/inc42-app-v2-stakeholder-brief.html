INC42 App · Product · v2

Get people from one brief to a daily habit

A pass across the whole first-time journey (welcome, brief, explore, company, and the moment right after finishing), built to close the gap between what the app promises and what the screen actually delivers.

Owner: Product Target release: 31 Aug (may move to first week of Sep) Status: scope for review

The app's whole pitch is simple: cut the noise, get your day in a few minutes instead of scrolling the internet for it. Right now, once someone is actually inside the app, the product doesn't consistently make good on that. Nothing tells them what they just got. Explore and Company feel like separate, less-finished apps bolted onto the brief. And the screen right after finishing celebrates a streak but never says the thing that got them here in the first place.

v2 is not a re-skin. It's a rebuild of that path: open, understand what you got, come back tomorrow, end to end.

What's changing, in detail

Same thirteen items, with the reasoning behind each.

01

Walkthrough

Today the guided tour is built directly into the app's code, so every wording or step change needs a new app release.

  • Move it onto Customer.io's native tour feature, so it can be updated any time without a new app build
  • This is purely about making updates easier. It is not a response to any drop-off numbers, which is a separate, real problem covered in the Welcome screen and onboarding items
  • Also fix onboarding sequencing already flagged in QA: notification-permission prompt timing, sector-selection validation
02

Welcome screen

The welcome screen today doesn't clearly convey the problem we solve or the solution we offer, so the app's value doesn't land in the first few seconds.

  • Redesign the visual to make the pitch more compelling and obvious up front

Holding, not building: the homepage banner. There's no usage data yet to design against, so we're parking it this cycle instead of guessing.

03

Brief card

The brief card today reads as one generic story rather than a curated set, and doesn't feel personalized.

  • Redesign the card with a few visual directions already explored, run them as an A/B test, and keep whichever performs best
  • Fix the contradictory card copy, and give the summary and the full article separate, clear calls to action
  • Add a cap so one topic can't dominate a single edition

Separate track: sector-based imagery is a distinct, unconfirmed idea, not part of this redesign yet. It also needs real sector tags to work from, which most articles don't have today.

04

Resuming an unfinished brief

If someone starts a brief but doesn't finish it, opening the app again looks exactly like they never started at all. There's no way to pick up where they left off.

  • Show progress and a clear "continue" option on the card once someone has started a brief, instead of the same "open today's brief" state every time
  • A few treatments already explored for this; direction is settled, exact one still being finalized
05

Full-article return path

When someone taps into the full article from a brief card, they often don't come back to finish the rest of that day's brief. Two data pulls disagree on exactly how often, so we're not quoting a number, but the fix is worth building regardless.

  • Add a clear way back into the brief after reading the full article, instead of the exit being the last step
06

Completion moment

Finishing a brief today only shows a streak count. It never tells the person what they actually got: that they're caught up, and didn't need to spend time scrolling elsewhere for news.

  • Lead the finish screen with that message, before the streak
  • Ask for an app rating right here, since it's the moment people feel best about the app, not after they've left it
07

Explore, layout

Explore feels flat and monotonous today: same layout, same order, nothing about it feels considered or personalized.

  • Show companies and stories based on what's actually moving (funding, hiring, and similar signals), not just what's most recent

Still early: this is being tested on real traffic first, not shipped as a full rebuild yet.

08

Explore, search

About 1 in 3 searches on Explore return nothing. A separate problem from the layout above, and a separate fix.

  • Investigate and fix. Likely a backend indexing issue, not a UI problem.
09

Explore › Company

Company pages don't make it easy to go deeper. Funding, financials and key people all sit behind separate taps today, so most people stop after the first section.

  • Bring financials and key people into one connected view instead of separate taps
  • Make search the primary way into the Companies tab, instead of a discovery scroll
  • Show funding amount and valuation directly on the "recently funded" widget
10

App-update prompt

There's no way today to prompt existing users to update to a newer version of the app.

  • Add a simple, dismissable prompt letting people know a new version is available, shown before the brief opens
  • Cannot be a forced update on iOS right now, since the current version has no update path yet
11

Store review prompt

There's no way today to ask users for a rating inside the app, on either platform. At this install count, a couple of early low ratings would set the visible score for months.

  • Ask for a rating only from people who've had a good experience (a few completed briefs, no crashes), not from everyone
  • Android uses Play's native review prompt. iOS uses Apple's equivalent, which is capped at 3 times a year with no way to know if the person actually rated.
12

Bug fixes

A set of smaller known issues, folded into this release rather than tracked separately.

  • Push notifications aren't delivering at all today despite people opting in. Fixing the root cause and moving to 1 or 2 relevant alerts a day instead of none.
  • Explore's filter popping open on its own instead of on request
  • Changing a filter tab not resetting scroll to top
  • Opening a brief story from a notification not returning properly
  • Double tap to exit, to stop accidental app exits
  • A save and rate race condition, where fast taps silently drop the save
  • iOS back button going dead after a few cards
  • Streak icon occasionally showing a dash instead of the count
  • Some icons intermittently failing to load
  • Plus a backlog of about 20 more items already logged
13

Dark mode

  • User requirement. Full dark theme across the app, already confirmed in scope for this release.

Experiments running alongside this

Nothing above ships as a single irreversible bet. Most of it is A/B tested, and copy or config changes are moved onto Customer.io so they don't need an app release to change.

One honest caveat: at about 189 users, none of these A/B tests reach statistical significance on their own. Treat this layer as release safety and a kill switch first, and directional signal second, not as a guarantee we'll have a clear verdict by launch.
