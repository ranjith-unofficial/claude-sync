## **The vision**

> ## **One Inc42: the intelligence engine for India's startup economy.**

> One product, one identity, one membership – content, data, community and AI together. Free for everyone building. Known by name at the core. Convened in rooms admission cannot buy.

In five lines:

1. Reach everyone building India's startup economy – free, open, amplified by social and video.  
2. Know the core by name – one registration, one identity, every surface.  
3. Deepen the core into members – content, data, community and AI in one membership that funds the product itself.  
4. Convene the best of them – in rooms that are earned, never bought.  
5. The market pays for the room – and the room pays for the engine.

## **The world as it is**

Ask five people what Inc42 is.

The founder says *"the startup news site – I read it every day."* The D2C brand owner says *"the summit – I was on that stage in August."* The growth lead says *"the Instagram page with the funding carousels."* The investor says *"the funding tracker."* The student says *"I follow them on LinkedIn."*

All of them are right. All of them are seeing a fragment. Five doors into the same house – and the house treats every door like a different building. The reader isn't known to the summit. The attendee isn't known to the database. The follower isn't known to anyone at all.

**And here is the irony we have lived with for twelve years:** we know everything about this economy, and nothing about the person asking. We can tell you every round a company ever raised, every pivot, every exit – we hold the record of the whole ecosystem. But the founder reading that record at dawn – alone, before the rest of the world is awake – the operator saving it, the attendee applauding it? Strangers. We can tell you everything about India's startup economy – except who's asking.

The hardest rooms are the ones you sit in by yourself. We have been sitting in millions of them, every morning, for twelve years – saying nothing.

So the problem, in one sentence – the reader, the attendee, the follower, all of them – is the sentence this vision exists to delete:

> "I read you every day. I sat in your audience. I share your posts. And you still don't know I exist."

The same is true from our side, at scale: millions arrive every year through five different doors, and leave – anonymous. The few we can name, we cannot describe. The rooms we convene are curated by hand from pools our own products barely feed. Three businesses, one audience, no relationship. The waste is the opportunity.

## **One member, ten years – and her Tuesday**

She found Inc42 in a hostel room – free, like it is for everyone – reading before the rest of the world was awake. It stayed free through her first job, her first idea, her first pitch. The day she incorporated, she signed up – one identity, given once – and claimed her company's profile.

Now it's a Tuesday, a few years on. She reads the Brief over chai – the only twenty minutes she gets. A story on quick-commerce margins carries her competitor's card – she taps it, and because Inc42 knows her, the profile opens against her watchlist: two funding alerts, and the comparison she saved last month. She asks the corpus one question – *who else is running dark stores under ₹50 Cr revenue?* – and the answer cites the record. Thursday she sits in an eight-person room with founders at her own stage: she applied once, was invited, and never bought a ticket – and the room is the first place building hasn't felt lonely. On the last slide of her fundraising deck: an Inc42 selection – earned, never bought.

Ten years a reader, two years a member, one Thursday in the room. The free layer was never the failure of the model. It was the beginning of every member we have.

And across the table from her ecosystem, the other side of the engine: a logistics company's growth head – a member too – queried the same record on Monday to find the fifty D2C brands crossing the shipping-volume threshold his product serves. His company sponsors the summit her cohort attends – and what his CMO bought was not a hall with a headcount, but a room we could prove: who exactly was in it, at what level, confirmed.

Today each of those moments takes a different product, and none of the products know each other. Soon they are one product that knows one person – because she wanted it to.

## **What Inc42 becomes**

The place where India's startup ecosystem is engaged – not merely informed. Informed is a pageview; engaged is a relationship: identified, described, active, and moving between reading, looking up, asking, belonging and meeting – without ever changing company.

We already hold the three assets this requires, and we are the only ones who do: the ecosystem's attention (the largest startup-media audience in the country), its record (the database and twelve years of editorial archive), and its room (the summits, the IPs, the club). Our nearest counterpart runs its news and data properties with zero links between them. What no one holds – what we have never held – is a single relationship with the person standing in all that traffic. This vision is that relationship.

And binding the three assets is the moat we have always had: the brand. Inc42 is how the ecosystem sees itself – twelve years of being its voice, its scoreboard and its stage. That is why the record is believed, why an invitation means something, and why selection can work as a credential at all. The brand has carried this company from the start; this vision gives it something it has never had – a relationship to stand on, not just a reputation. Growing that brand is not a department's job inside this plan; it is a stated focus of the whole company, and every stage of the engine below both borrows it and must repay it.

## **The engine**

Each stage feeds the next. The last stage pays for the first.

|  | Stage | Scale | What it runs on |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| 1 | Reach – free, open journalism; social and video carry it far beyond our own pages | Millions | The journalism the ecosystem already trusts – never walled, never metered |
| 2 | Know – signing up opens the full database; we ask who you are, once | Tens of thousands | A free account – identity, never payment |
| 3 | Deepen – one membership: content \+ data \+ community \+ AI | Thousands | Funds the entire product and tech team |
| 4 | Convene – curated rooms and IPs; standing conferred, relationships deepened | Hundreds | Selection – the judgment the brand is trusted for |
| 5 | Pay for it all – the market buys the room and, for the first time, proof of who is in it | The primary business | Sponsors and enablers who need to reach stages 2–4 |

Three loops spin the engine faster. The credential loop: the rooms produce our most credible stories, and social and video turn them back into reach – *room → film → reach → nominations → room* – so standing becomes visible, and visible standing draws the next cohort. The belonging loop: the rooms are not only the business – they are where the engine's relationships actually deepen. People who meet in an Inc42 room stay for the people, and the community that runs between rooms is retention infrastructure, not an event by-product. Belonging, not billing, is why year two happens. The engine room: AI does the heavy lifting behind the scenes – matching, sorting, personalising, verifying – so serving the core deeply never needs an army, which is what makes *free for the millions, deeper for the core* affordable.

And the engine's fuel is trust. Every stage borrows the Inc42 name – the story is read because the byline is ours, the invitation is accepted because the name on it means something – and every stage must repay it: journalism earns the trust, the record substantiates it, the room embodies it. Break it at any stage and the whole engine slows.

This engine is not a new idea wearing new words – it is the brand's own locked architecture set in motion: journalism drives, intelligence fuels, community convenes. The vision and the message are the same thing at two altitudes.

And at the engine's apex, deliberately apart: Griffin. The club carries its own name by design – *the exclusivity is carried by the independence* – an Inc42 Group initiative that borrows the group's standing and returns the proof at the top of the ladder: that in this ecosystem, selection is worth more than access. It is not a tier of the membership and not a destination of the funnel. It is what the engine makes possible.

## **What we are – and are not**

- Curated, not exhaustive. Chasing every company in every spreadsheet is a race the databases run at a loss. Our judgment is the product – as a newsroom it always has been. We will know the companies that matter, and we will be the reason they are known.  
- Earned, not sold. Admission, selection and standing can be earned at Inc42 and cannot be purchased – which is precisely why they will be worth putting on a deck. The clubs sell rooms with no record behind them; we confer standing that the record substantiates.  
- Membership, not paywall. The journalism stays open, for everyone, always. The paywalls sell articles in a market that has priced them near zero; our members are not paying to read – they belong.

## **Why now**

The rails finally exist. One login, one billing rail, consolidated surfaces – and nearly everything the membership needs is already built. Unification is now a shipping problem, not an engineering one.

AI just made the deep end economical. Qualification, curation, personalisation and composition-measurement can run as automations. Five years ago this vision required a headcount the economics could not carry; today it requires adoption.

Both wars around us are the wrong ones. On the data front, the incumbent is discounting to defend completeness – a race that loses money even when you win it. On the media front, everyone is fighting for pageviews in an ad market that keeps shrinking the prize. Nobody on either front is building the relationship – the position this vision claims sits empty.

And the room has already voted. Convening has grown from roughly a quarter to nearly two-thirds of the sponsorship book in three years. The engine's last stage is proven and hungry for a qualified pool. The rest of the engine is what we now build.

## **FY31 – what success looks like**

- Around 50,000 of the ecosystem's core professionals – of a core market of roughly 70–180K professionals today, growing toward 120–280K by FY31 – hold a standing, identified, active relationship with Inc42, not a pageview.  
- A member is one person everywhere – article, database, app, newsletter, room.  
- The room is stronger on every axis – worth more per event (because who attended is proven, not promised), bought by more sponsors, and no longer resting on a handful of them.  
- The membership pays for the entire product and tech team several times over – and revenue never drives its decisions: no feature ships to make the number, because keeping the core engaged is its job, and growth beyond that is a free win.  
- And the brand is what it has always been, made stronger – the ecosystem's default lens on itself: the name founders want on their deck, the stage they want to stand on, the record they trust – now resting on tens of thousands of real relationships, not reputation alone.

## **What we refuse to build**

- A data-completeness contest – the record serves the relationship; it is not a race to win.  
- A paywall in any form – open journalism is the top of the engine, not a leak in it.  
- A second product line for a second audience – one ladder, one product; the buyer segments emerge, they are not marketed to.  
- Purchasable selection – the day inclusion can be bought, the credential is worthless and the rooms with it.

## **How this could be wrong**

Four honest ways, named before anyone else names them. Today's subscribers may not move to the new membership. A membership may not keep people where a content subscription and a data subscription both failed – this is the product bet itself. Sponsors may not pay more for proof of who was in the room – they may keep buying on relationships alone. And the AI engine room only holds the economics if the team actually adopts the tools – the tools themselves are the easy part. Each of these has a test in the strategy with a pass/fail bar agreed in advance – so a miss redirects us early, before the money is spent, instead of being argued away after it.

## **What stands still**

This document – the statement, the member's claim, the engine, the refusals – is fixed for the horizon: re-ratified annually against results, not rewritten. The strategy beneath it is *expected* to change as evidence lands; every bet there carries a hypothesis and a bar, and revision there is the system working. The vision has an evangelist whose job is repetition – in the corridor, on the stage, in the plan – because a vision that lives in a file is a strategy document with better adjectives.

> Free for the millions. Known at the core. Deeper by membership. Convened by invitation. Paid for by the room.
