# **Product Trainee**

# ***Inc42 · Delhi*** 

## **About Inc42**

Inc42 is India’s largest media and intelligence platform on the startup economy – spanning journalism, **Datalabs** (India’s largest free startup database, with 75,000+ companies), our flagship Editorial IPs (AI Summit, D2C & Retail Summit, FAST42, D2C Retreat), and **Griffin**, India’s most curated private founder club.

## **Why this role exists**

We’re looking for someone who can combine product thinking, strong analytical skills, and AI fluency to help us identify problems, turn insights into action, and get solutions shipped. You’ll work closely with the Product & Marketing team across product analytics, experimentation, product development, and AI-powered workflows.

## **What you’ll own**		

* Product Discovery: Talk to users, conduct user interviews, understand pain points, and translate insights into product opportunities.

* Product Building: Work with design and engineering to take products and features from problem definition and ideation through PRD, development, launch, and iteration.

* Product Analytics: Analyse funnels, cohorts, user behaviour, product metrics and tools like BigQuery, PostHog, GA4, Mixpanel, or Amplitude.

* Experimentation: Form hypotheses, design experiments, measure outcomes, and use data to decide what to build next.

* Product Improvement: Continuously identify opportunities to improve the user experience, engagement, and retention across Inc42's products.

* AI & Automation: Build and improve AI workflows, n8n automations, and prompts that reduce manual work across Editorial and Product.

* 0-to-1 Initiatives: Support new product ideas from early research and validation through to building, launching, and learning from real users.

* Take on ambiguous, high-priority problems where user understanding, data, product thinking, and execution need to come together.

## **Who you are**

* At least 6 months of experience in Product, Analytics, Growth role.

* Strong analytical judgement, you can separate meaningful signals from noise.

* AI-fluent and able to evaluate, debug, and improve AI outputs and prompts.

* Clear written communicator who can turn analysis into actionable recommendations.

* Execution-oriented, you don't just find problems; you help get the fix shipped.

* Curious, proactive, and comfortable working with ambiguity.

We’re open in the background. Media, B2B SaaS, fintech, consumer, dev tools- what matters is the operator chops, the AI fluency, and the taste.

* Front seat at India’s largest startup media company \- high autonomy, direct founder access, and work that compounds
