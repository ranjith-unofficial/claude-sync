## What this document deliberately does NOT contain — and AskInc42 must never state

- **Any price, price range, "starts from", pass/tier name, discount code, struck-through price, or seat availability.** For any pricing question: redirect to that IP's website.
- **Any refund or cancellation terms.** One response only, for every such question, for every property: *"I can't help with refunds or cancellations — please reach out through the Contact Us page and the team will pick it up."* Refund terms differ per property and are conditional; that awareness exists here **only** so nothing gets invented, never to be explained. This holds even if the user asks a second time.
- Sponsor commercials, partnership rates, or delegate targets.
- Internal notes, unlaunched IPs, or anything not published on a live Inc42 site.
- Any technology, vendor, model, or internal process detail.

**Every entry must be read out with its `Status` first.** Several editions below have already concluded. Never describe a concluded event in the present tense.

### 2.1 The D2C & Retail Summit

- **Current name:** The D2C & Retail Summit 2026 by Inc42. *(Formerly "The D2C Summit" — `inc42.com/d2c-summit` now redirects to the event site.)*
- **Edition:** 7th (6th in-person).
- **Status:** Upcoming — **19 August 2026**.
- **Where:** The Leela Ambience, Gurugram.
- **What it is:** India's D2C and retail conference. 2026 theme: *Commerce In The Age Of AI & 10-Min Delivery* — omnichannel infrastructure, AI-led execution, capital-efficient scaling, Bharat-ready strategies.
- **Who it's for:** D2C and retail founders and CXOs, D2C/retail investors, angels with 10+ D2C/retail investments, and enabler/service-provider leaders.
- **Scale:** 40+ speakers · 15+ sessions incl. firesides and panels · 4 workshops · 300+ handpicked participants (600+ cited in event comms).

### 2.2 Inc42 AI Summit

- **Current name:** Inc42 AI Summit 2026.
- **Edition:** 3rd. *(Earlier editions ran as the GenAI Summit.)*
- **Status:** 🔴 **Concluded — held 28 May 2026.** The next edition has not been announced.
- **Where:** Sheraton Grand, Whitefield, Bengaluru.
- **What it is:** India's largest invite-only AI summit — applied, production-ready AI for Indian market conditions. Two stages (strategy/scale and a hands-on build stage) plus workshops. Topics include the AI-led fintech stack, enterprise AI contracts, multilingual AI, and AI infrastructure.

- **Scale (2026):** 600+ attendees · 50+ speakers · 25+ sessions · 4 workshops.

- **Website:** `events.inc42.com/ai-summit`

### 2.3 D2CX Converge

- **Current name:** D2CX Converge — Season 2, by Inc42. Presenting partner: **Shadowfax**.
- **Status:** Ongoing — **next city: Pune, 10 September 2026**. Concluded editions: Hyderabad (Nov 2025), Bengaluru and Mumbai (Mar 2026), Jaipur (Apr 2026), Ahmedabad (May 2026), Indore (Jul 2026), Kolkata (Jul 2026).
- **What it is:** a founder-first, city-by-city meetup series for early-stage D2C founders — operator-led tactics on scaling a consumer brand, plus investor and partner facetime. Format: ~90 minutes of firesides and panels, then 60–90 minutes of curated networking and peer circles.
- **Who it's for:** D2C brand founders with a live website and online sales, up to ₹10 Cr annual online revenue.
- **Access:** **Free** to apply and attend. Seats are limited per city; selection is based on stage, category and intent fit.
- **How to apply:** application form linked from the programme page.
- **Website:** `inc42.com/d2cx-converge-season-2` (also `inc42.com/d2cx-converge`)

### 2.6 FAST42

- **Current name:** FAST42, D2C Edition 2027, presented by Inc42.
- **Edition:** 6th. *(The 2026 list has already been published; the live cycle is 2027.)*
- **Status:** Applications for the 2027 list close **December 2026**; the list is unveiled **February 2027**.
- **What it is:** Inc42's ranking of India's 42 fastest-growing D2C brands, ranked by revenue growth between FY23 and FY25.
- **Eligibility:** private, for-profit, independent Indian companies · own ecommerce website as a key sales channel · incorporated after 1 January 2020 · minimum ₹1 Cr revenue in FY23 · minimum ₹10 Cr in FY25 · maximum ₹150 Cr in any financial year.
- **Access:** **No application fee.**
- **Partners:** powered by Nitro, Avalara and Kentrix; associate partners BIK and Rukam Capital.
- **What ranked brands get:** an interactive ranking on Inc42, inclusion in the coffee-table book, employer-branding visibility, business and investor exposure, and event invitations.
- **Related:** the **FAST42 Conclave** — a half-day launch event for each list, convening 150+ D2C founders, investors, operators and ecosystem builders alongside the 42 ranked founders, with firesides, panels, closed-door conversations and structured networking.
- **Eligibility rulings:** state the published criteria; never tell a user whether they personally qualify.
- **Website:** the current FAST42 edition page on `inc42.com`

### 2.7 D2CX Runway

- **Current name:** D2CX Runway, by Inc42 and **Rukam Capital**.
- **Status:** 🔴 **The 2026 cohort has concluded** — applications ran 30 March–8 May, virtual bootcamp 20 June–25 July, physical residency 2–7 August, Demo Day 7 August 2026. The next cohort has not been announced.
- **What it is:** a high-intensity hybrid accelerator for D2C brands entering their next growth phase — a virtual bootcamp followed by a physical residency in Delhi NCR, with a Demo Day. Anchored on three pillars: brand building, operational excellence, and capital readiness. Backed by a **₹10 Cr investment pool**.
- **Cohort size:** 15 startups shortlisted through a deeper evaluation process. The inaugural cohort had 14 brands across fashion, F&B, babycare, jewellery and automotive.
- **Who it's for:** D2C / consumer-tech brands (BPC, F&B, fashion, health and similar) with ₹1–8 Cr annual revenue, registered after 1 January 2020, bootstrapped / seed / pre-Series A, with a live product, consistent month-on-month growth, and full-time founders able to attend the residency.
- ⚠️ **Known source inconsistency:** the programme page is titled a "4-week accelerator" while the body describes a 6-week virtual bootcamp plus a 6-day residency, and press coverage has called it both four and seven weeks. **Do not state a duration until Ranjith resolves this.** Describe the structure (virtual bootcamp → physical residency → Demo Day) instead.
- **Website:** `inc42.com/d2cx-runway`

## 5. Maintenance

- **Weekly review** — check every entry above against its live site: edition, dates, status, application windows, eligibility. Update `Verified` date at the top.
- **Off-cycle triggers** — a new IP launches, an edition is announced, applications open or close, an event concludes, or an IP is renamed.
- **On rename** — keep the old name as an alias so a user asking about "the D2C Summit" or "GenAI Summit" still resolves.
- **Never delete a concluded edition.** Mark it concluded and keep its dates; deleting it leaves a retrieval gap, and the dates are what let the model answer "has it happened yet?" correctly.
- **Check the subdomain, not just `inc42.com`.** The CTO Summit was missed in the first audit because it lives on `events.inc42.com` and isn't listed on the main events page. Sweep both hosts each week.
