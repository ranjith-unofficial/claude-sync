# **One Inc42 – the product vision**

**v1 · 2026-08-11 · Desk:** platform · Serves the company vision (VISION.md) – this document is the product's share of it, written to give the product roadmap a stable direction while the strategy (ONE-INC42-STRATEGY.md) revises beneath it. Confidentiality: internal; directional figures only.

---

## **What the product is**

> The product is the engine's memory: it is how Inc42 knows someone, and how knowing them compounds. The journalism reaches millions and the rooms convene hundreds – the product is everything between: the identity, the membership, and the intelligence that turn an anonymous reader into a known, engaged member the rooms can draw on.

The company vision's engine has five stages. The product owns stages 2 and 3, and serves 4 and 5:

| Stage | The product's job |
| :---- | :---- |
| 2 · Know | One free account, one identity, every surface. Role and company asked once, at signup – because registration is the only way we ever learn who anyone is |
| 3 · Deepen | One membership – content, data, community and AI together – which sustains the product organisation, and whose real output is an engaged, described, active core |
| 4–5 · Serve | Feed and enlarge the qualified pool the rooms curate from – and make the composition of any room provable, which is what the primary business sells |

## **The member's claim**

*One identity through which you track your market, look up any company, ask the corpus, meet your peers, and become visible to the rooms where the ecosystem meets – things that today take five disconnected products, none of which know each other.*

## **The north star**

> ### **Qualified Identified Actives (QIA) – people with a known role, inside the core professional market, who took a qualifying action in a rolling 30-day window *(action list and exclusions: strategy §5)*.**

*Identified* excludes the anonymous 98% · *qualified* excludes the aspirant volume · *active* excludes the dormant. Trajectory (FY convention, horizon FY31): 15,000 (FY28) → 35–40,000 (FY30) → \~50,000 (FY31) – enrichment in the base case; final calibration on the 30-day baseline pull. FY27-H2 asks the line for no revenue – it is the engine-building half. Membership revenue is welcome to grow – but it is never bought at the audience's expense: no feature ships to make the number, no engagement mechanism is degraded to monetise, and the line's first duty is sustaining the product organisation that builds it. Beyond that, growth is a free win – not a target anyone is chased on.

## **The ladder**

*The ladder below is the feasibility-proven hypothesis space – the shipped offering and its entitlements are derived from engagement evidence (which mechanisms the engaged audience actually uses), not launched from this table.*

|  | Price | Fence |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Free | ₹0 | Identity. Articles open and unwalled, always; profiles and database free but registration-gated |
| T1 | ₹4,999–14,999/yr *(price point within the band, renewal treatment, and monthly-vs-annual: TBD – experimented or discovered)* | Utility: alerts, saved searches, capped exports, Deal Digest, reports, community, uncapped Ask |
| T2 | ₹25,000–50,000/yr | Same audience, more depth. Volume meters trigger the upgrade; hard capability is the wall – contact information, financials, cap tables, uncapped exports, analyst credits |
| T3 | *deferred* | Invite-only, capacity-capped rooms. Ticketing is the funnel into it. Needs its own validation |

Griffin is not on this ladder – it is the group's independently-branded pinnacle, and the product never funnels into it.

## **How the product works – outcome fixed, mechanism open**

The outcome is identify → activate → retain. No mechanism is privileged in advance – the app, the newsletters, the community, the entity card and the follow graph compete on QIA, and the winners get investment:

|  | Owed design | Current candidates |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Identify | Unified onboarding – one persona \+ role ask, captured once, reused everywhere | Registration-gated profiles · app onboarding · newsletter signup |
| Activate | Cross-surface discovery – the reading path leads somewhere | In-body entity card (a variant ships) · app explore · Ask as a layer, never a destination |
| Retain | Lifecycle journeys – state-based, never stage-based | The Datalabs Weekly · alerts (to build) · community · the Brief · the rooms \+ the community between them |

## **What the product refuses to be**

- A paywall – journalism is never metered; members belong, they do not "unlock."  
- A second product for a second audience – one ladder; the buyer segments emerge from who upgrades.  
- A data-completeness product – the record serves the relationship; the entity layer's job is qualification, not revenue.  
- The app as the strategy – the app is one representation of the product, gated on proving itself like every other mechanism.  
- A seller of selection – the product may make members *visible* to the rooms' curation; inclusion is never purchasable.

## **How the roadmap derives from this**

1. Every roadmap item names the hypothesis it validates and what that unlocks – the sequence lives in the company strategy's single ledger (A1–A13), not here.  
2. Instrument before launch – no comparable anywhere publishes a renewal rate; we will only know ours if measurement ships first. One identity spine across the analytics and lifecycle stacks is Horizon-1 work.  
3. The migration probe (A1) starts now – it needs nothing built, informs the offering's design, decides FY28's shape, and earns revenue while it runs.

**Stability:** this direction changes on ledger evidence, not on opinion. The strategy beneath it is expected to revise; this page should survive several of those revisions – and if it cannot, that is a finding, not a formality.
