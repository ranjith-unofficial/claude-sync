# **Competitive Feature and Pricing Benchmark: Tracxn, VCCircle/VCCEdge, and Venture Intelligence vs. DataLabs**

Inc42’s DataLabs platform operates as a specialized intelligence layer within the Indian startup and investment ecosystem, offering over 75,000 company profiles, 3,000 investor profiles, and 20,000 funding round records. DataLabs currently monetizes through an individual "Pro" subscription at approximately ₹1,499 per month and a 5-seat "Team" tier at ₹50,000 plus GST annually. DataLabs' company profile snapshot currently displays five baseline fields: primary sector, founding year, headquarters city, founders, and total funding amount.  
To scale DataLabs into an institutional-grade deal sourcing and due diligence platform, Inc42 requires a granular understanding of the feature sets, dataset depth, search mechanics, export capabilities, and pricing structures of its primary domestic competitors. This report profiles three India-focused platforms: Tracxn, VCCircle (alongside its data platform VCCEdge), and Venture Intelligence.

## **Tracxn**

Tracxn Technologies Limited, headquartered in Bengaluru, operates a global private market data platform tracking over 7.7 million entities across 3,000 sectors and 55,400 business model taxonomies1.

### **1\. Full Pricing Ladder**

Tracxn structures its commercial offerings around a freemium preview model, quote-based enterprise seat licenses, and specialized API data solution packs2.

* **Tracxn Lite (Personal / Free Tier)**: Available at ₹0 / $0 with no credit card required upon registration, observed on the official pricing page (tracxn.com/pricing) in March 20262. This tier provides restricted preview access to the global database of 7.7 million companies, 1.8 million funding rounds, and 290,000 investors2. Access is constrained by monthly usage limits that reset on the first day of each calendar month2. It limits users to 1 report download per day and 200 export credits per month2. It explicitly excludes API packs, custom taxonomy mapping, live deals, and multi-user collaboration2.  
* **Tracxn Premium (Commercial / Enterprise Tier)**: Marked explicitly as quote-based ("Contact Sales") on official platform documentation updated in 2026 (tracxn.com/pricing and G2 vendor statements)2. Final pricing is on-demand and tailored to team size, data volume, and deployment requirements2. Conflicting legacy figures appear across third-party software review aggregators; for example, Crozdesk and Techimply listings dated between 2023 and 2025 reflect older cached packages such as a Single User tier at $500–$550 per month billed annually, a Small Team tier (3 users) at $1,100 per month billed quarterly, and a Group tier (7 users) at $2,200 per month billed quarterly6. However, official 2026 documentation confirms all active enterprise plans require a direct sales quote2. The Premium tier includes unrestricted database searches, complete taxonomy mapping, Deal Flow CRM, Portfolio Tracker, custom report generation, 24/7 support (Live Chat, WhatsApp, Email), and the MyAnalyst research desk (which includes 1 free custom analyst query per user per month)2.  
* **Data Solutions Packs (APIs / Data Dumps)**: Provided as quote-based enterprise add-ons observed on w.tracxn.com/offerings/data-solutions/pricing2. Legacy documentation lists flat-rate unlimited API usage at $4,000 per month billed annually, with extra export credit packs available at $1,000 per 10,000 credits7. Data Solutions are split into two licensing frameworks:  
  * *Internal Usage License Packs*: Includes "Full Access Packs" granting unrestricted database access for internal analytics, and "Limited Access Packs" capped either by profile fetch limits or targeted dataset slices (e.g., India Fintech)2.  
  * *Commercial Redistribution License Packs*: Built for organizations embedding Tracxn data into external commercial applications, featuring volume-based pricing tied to the unique number of company profiles accessed2.

### **2\. Company Profile Data Fields**

A full Tracxn company profile, as documented on its company profile product page (w.tracxn.com/offerings/company-profiles) and sample preview pages (such as tracxn.com/d/companies/vccedge/\_\_Zy7aO83o53DfVxe31OjdNazvtYo9DAGH9YTHtfNDoic), renders a broad set of operational, financial, and market fields9:

* **Profile Overview & Essentials**: Company Name, Legal Entity Name, Corporate Identification Number (CIN), Incorporation Date / Founding Year, Headquarters Location (City, State, Country), Active Operating Status, Web URL, Short Business Description2.  
* **Taxonomy & Business Model**: Primary Feed/Sector, Business Model Classification (mapping revenue streams, customer segments, and delivery channels), Taxonomy Category Tags across 55,400+ classifications2.  
* **Funding & Valuation Details**: Total Capital Raised, Round-by-Round Funding History, Transaction Dates, Pre-Money Valuation, Post-Money Valuation, Capital Raised Per Round, Lead Investors, Participating Investors, Valuation & Revenue Multiples, Investor Traction Signals2.  
* **Financial Performance**: Multi-year Income Statements, Balance Sheets, and Cash Flow Statements derived from Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) regulatory filings, Annual Revenue, EBITDA, Net Profit/Loss, Expenses Breakdown, Burn Rate Trends2.  
* **Cap Table & Equity Structure**: Equity Ownership Percentage Breakdown among Founders, ESOP Pools, Angel Investors, and Institutional Funds, Post-Round Shareholding Patterns, Founder Dilution History2.  
* **People & Organizational Signals**: Founder Profiles and Executive Bios (including past career history and social links), Board Members, Key Directors, Team Growth Trajectory, Historical Employee Count Trends2.  
* **Competitive Intelligence & Performance**: Instant Competitor Lists (automatically mapped by valuation, revenue, and team size), Market Share Signals, Web Traffic Analytics, Directional Mobile App Rank Trends2.  
* **Strategic Transactions & Exits**: Acquisitions Made, Strategic Investments, Exit Events (IPO details, acquisition valuation, exit dates)2.  
* **Documents & Media**: Company Pitch Decks, Regulatory Filings (MCA documents), Sector Research Reports, Verified News Highlights (funding events, M\&A, leadership changes)2.

Free/Lite account holders see a restricted preview version displaying basic headers, total funding, primary sector, and high-level news summaries; full cap tables, line-item financials, and downloadable filing documents require a paid Premium subscription2.

### **3\. Search and Filter Depth**

Tracxn provides a deep screening interface leveraging over 55,400 business model tags and 3,000 sector feeds2. Users can screen entities across 12 distinct filter dimensions explicitly observable on the platform:

> 1. Primary Sector / Feed2  
> 2. Business Model Taxonomy Tag2  
> 3. Total Funding Range2  
> 4. Funding Stage (Seed, Series A through E+, Late Stage)2  
> 5. Investor Participation / Investor Portfolio2  
> 6. Valuation Range (Pre-money / Post-money)2  
> 7. Annual Revenue Range2  
> 8. Employee Count / Employee Growth Percentage2  
> 9. Geographic Location (Country, State, City)2  
> 10. Growth Triggers and Signals (e.g., Generative AI, Spacetech, hiring spikes)2  
> 11. M\&A / Acquisition / Public Listing Status2  
> 12. Incorporation Date / Founding Year Range2

### **4\. Export and Integration**

* **Exports**: PDF exports (pre-formatted One-Pagers, Due Diligence Packs, Competitor Overviews) and Excel/CSV structured data downloads2. Export capabilities are gated by export credits: Lite accounts receive 200 credits per month, while Premium accounts receive larger monthly allocations or can purchase add-on credit bundles ($1,000 per 10,000 credits)2.  
* **API Access**: Available via the Data Solutions module, providing REST APIs for internal analytics or external commercial integration (gated to paid API add-on tiers)2.  
* **Browser Extensions & Plugins**: Offers Google Chrome and Mozilla Firefox browser extensions that surface company profiles directly while visiting target corporate websites, alongside a dedicated Google Sheets Plugin2.  
* **CRM Integrations**: Features a native Deal Flow CRM, Portfolio Tracker, DevRev integration, and direct workflow exports to external CRMs such as Salesforce and Pipedrive2.

### **5\. Editorial and News Content**

Tracxn does not operate a standalone journalism outlet, but it embeds automated news tracking and curated market reports directly alongside its profile data2. It regularly publishes sector and geographic newsletters, "Soonicorn & Unicorn Club" rankings, and market landscape studies2. Basic news summaries and headline alerts are accessible on the free Lite tier, whereas full historical news logs and in-depth sector research reports are gated to paid Premium subscribers2.

## **VCCircle and VCCEdge**

The Mosaic Digital ecosystem (a subsidiary of HT Media Limited) separates its service into two distinct offerings: VCCircle, an editorial financial news portal tracking private equity and venture capital, and VCCEdge, an institutional financial research platform covering over 393,000 private entities, 73,000 companies with audited financial records, and 88,000 tracked deals13.

### **1\. Full Pricing Ladder**

#### **VCCircle Subscription Tiers (Editorial & Deal News Paywall)**

Observed on the official subscription portal (subscription.vccircle.com) in March 2026:

* **Premium Tier**: ₹5,299 per year, billed annually15. Includes unlimited VCCircle premium news articles, daily and weekly market newsletters, a 20-year news archive, limited monthly market snapshot reports, and basic deal tracker custom alerts15.  
* **Pro Lite Tier**: ₹15,999 per year, billed annually15. Includes everything in Premium, plus exclusive PRO analysis articles, monthly market snapshots, sector deep-dive reports, complete deal tracker access with custom alerts, and a 10% discount on VCCircle PE/VC Summits15.  
* **Pro Tier**: ₹24,999 per year, billed annually15. Includes everything in Pro Lite, plus a 25% discount on PE/VC Summits, ad-free browsing, priority support, advance access to newsbreaks and analysis, and article teasers prior to public release15.  
* **Enterprise Tier**: Quote-based multi-seat team plan offering volume discounts up to 30% across subscription tiers, billed annually with assigned onboarding support15.

#### **VCCEdge Database Tiers (Financial Research Platform)**

Observed on the VCCEdge product page (vccedge.com/research-consulting) and terms of service (vccedge.com/terms-and-conditions):

* **Standard Domestic Plan**: ₹2,75,000 per year, billed annually18. Publicly listed for domestic research, consulting, and investment team deployment18. Provides full access to private company profiles, audited MCA financial statements, deal histories across PE/VC/M\&A, and screening tools14.  
* **Custom Enterprise / International Plan**: Quote-based / Contact Sales (sales@vccedge.com), billed annually18. Designed for international institutions, larger team configurations, and custom data delivery requirements18.

### **2\. Company Profile Data Fields**

VCCEdge focuses heavily on corporate compliance filings and financial statements sourced directly from the MCA18. Observed on the VCCEdge platform features landing page (vccedge.com) and public preview pages, a complete company profile renders the following fields:

* **Basic Corporate Snapshot**: Company Name, Date of Incorporation, Corporate Identification Number (CIN), Registered Office Address/Location, Industry Sector, Advanced Taxonomy Tags3.  
* **Audited Financial Statements**: Up to 5 years of detailed Profit & Loss (P\&L) Statements, Balance Sheets, and Cash Flow Statements derived from over 1 million MCA audited filings14.  
* **Pre-Calculated Financial Ratios**: Multi-year liquidity ratios, profitability margins, solvency metrics, Debt-to-Equity, Return on Equity, and CAGR growth calculations18.  
* **Capital Structure & Shareholding**: Cap Table details, Shareholding Structure, Promoter vs. Institutional equity splits, Director/Management changes, Legal Filings, Share Capital alterations18.  
* **Deal History & Transaction Analytics**: Detailed histories across Private Equity, Venture Capital, M\&A (Domestic, Inbound, Outbound), Equity Capital Market (ECM), and Debt Transactions14. Fields include Stake Percentage Transacted, Disclosed Deal Value, Post-Money Valuation, and Participating Advisors (Investment Banks and Law Firms)14.  
* **People & Governance**: Profiles of Board Directors, Key Management Personnel, and Executive Networks across 295,000 listed professionals14.  
* **Key Developments**: A log of over 344,000 corporate events tracking board shifts, litigation notes, office expansions, and strategic alliances14.  
* **Peer Group Benchmarking**: Automated peer company financial comparison models and portfolio monitoring tools20.

Free visitors see high-level corporate headers and transaction summaries; detailed audited financial statements, ratio calculations, and complete cap tables require an active VCCEdge subscription18.

### **3\. Search and Filter Depth**

VCCEdge utilizes an AI/NLP-driven search engine built on an Advanced Taxonomy Framework14. Users can search and filter across 10 distinct dimensions explicitly documented on the platform:

> 1. Deal Transaction Type (PE, VC, M\&A, ECM, Debt)14  
> 2. Investment Stage (Seed, Series A through D, Pre-IPO)14  
> 3. Deal Value / Disclosed Ticket Size23  
> 4. Audited Financial Line Items (Revenue, EBITDA, Net Profit thresholds)18  
> 5. Pre-Calculated Financial Ratios18  
> 6. Sector Taxonomy Classification14  
> 7. Geographic Location / Transaction Flow (Domestic, Inbound, Outbound)18  
> 8. Advisory Firm Involvement (Investment Banks, Legal Counsel)14  
> 9. Deal Date Range23  
> 10. MCA Regulatory Entity Status18

### **4\. Export and Integration**

* **Exports**: Direct Excel (.xlsx) spreadsheet downloads containing financial models and pre-calculated ratio tables, alongside standardized PDF advisory summary reports18. Gated strictly to paid VCCEdge subscription licenses18.  
* **API Access**: Offered as a custom enterprise add-on for institutional subscribers (quote-based)18.  
* **Browser Extension**: Not listed / Not verified.  
* **CRM Integrations**: Supports custom data export routines for external CRM loading; features automated email alerts and customized "Deal Intelligence" digest deliveries18.

### **5\. Editorial and News Content**

VCCEdge operates in close synergy with VCCircle's editorial team15. While VCCEdge embeds real-time "Key Developments" directly onto company profile pages, VCCircle publishes daily financial news, original deal scoops, and long-form sector coverage14. Access to VCCircle's editorial content is strictly paywalled behind its ₹5,299 to ₹24,999 annual subscription ladder15. VCCEdge subscribers receive proprietary research publications, such as *India's Dealscape* (H1 and Annual Deals Reports)24.

## **Venture Intelligence**

Operated by TSJ Media Pvt. Ltd. in Chennai, Venture Intelligence is a long-standing private market platform in India, specializing in venture capital, private equity, M\&A transactions, and private company financials27.

### **1\. Full Pricing Ladder**

Venture Intelligence caters primarily to institutional private equity funds, venture capital firms, investment banks, and corporate strategy groups29.

* **PE-VC Deals & Company Financials Database (Core Platform)**: Quote-based / Custom Annual Subscription (bizdev@ventureintelligence.com), observed on official product pages (ventureintelligence.com/products/pe) in March 202629. Subscriptions are structured as 1-Year or 2-Year contracts, with a 10% discount offered on 2-year commitments30. Subscriptions grant access to the PE-VC Investments & Exits Database, Company Financials Search (CFS) covering 50,000+ private entities, M\&A Database, PE-Real Estate Database, Unicorn Tracker, and Advisory League Tables29.  
* **Competitive Intelligence Financial Summaries (A La Carte for Founders/SMEs)**: ₹5,000 for a crisp financial summary covering 3 specified private companies, plus ₹1,500 per additional company, observed on ventureintelligence.com/entrepreneurs33. Designed for founders seeking competitor P\&L statements without purchasing an institutional database license33.  
* **Deal Digest Showcase Insert**: ₹5,000 \+ GST per insertion33. Enables fundraising startups to publish their investment criteria directly into the *Deal Digest* newsletter distributed to institutional investors33.  
* **India Private Equity-Real Estate Directory**: Sold as a standalone annual spreadsheet directory for real estate developers and service providers seeking fund profiles34.

### **2\. Company Profile Data Fields**

Observed on the platform's product pages (ventureintelligence.com/products/pe and ventureintelligence.com/products), a full Venture Intelligence company profile presents the following fields:

* **Entity Snapshot**: Company Name, Brand Name, Primary Industry Sector, Sub-industry Classification, Business Description, Specialization/Differentiation Notes32.  
* **PE-VC Investment Transactions**: Historical Funding Rounds, Deal Dates, Capital Raised per Round, Round Stage (Seed, Series A through E+), Lead Investors, Participating Investors, Angel Investments, Accelerator/Incubation history27.  
* **Valuations & Pricing Multiples**: Pre-Money Valuation, Post-Money Valuation, Enterprise Valuation (EV), Implied Valuation Multiples (EV/Revenue, EV/EBITDA)30.  
* **Exits & Investor Return Analytics**: Exit Transactions (M\&A, Secondary Buyout, IPO), Exit Valuation, Realized Investor Return Multiples (Cash-on-Cash / MoIC), Fund IRR metrics (for completed exits)30.  
* **Detailed Audited Financials (CFS Module)**: Multi-year P\&L Statements, Balance Sheets, Cash Flow Statements, Interim Results, Financial Ratios, Common Size Analysis, and CAGR growth calculations for over 50,000 private entities32.  
* **Regulatory Debt Data (Index of Charges)**: Company filings submitted to the MCA, combined with "Index of Charges" tracking bank loans, venture debt instruments, open secured borrowings, and satisfied charges32.  
* **Cap Tables & Shareholding Breakdown**: Post-Deal Cap Tables updated after every transaction, detailing shareholding percentages across founders, key shareholders, and institutional funds27.  
* **Directories & Governance**: Profiles of Board Members and Supervisory Directors, Company Networks, PE/VC Investor Profiles, Portfolio Company tracking, LP Directory30.

Non-subscribers viewing preview pages see basic transaction announcements; complete audited financials, post-deal cap tables, exit IRRs, and index of charges data require an active institutional account31.

## **Strategic Feature Gap Analysis for DataLabs**

DataLabs currently provides five baseline fields on its company profile pages: sector, founding year, headquarters city, founders, and total funding amount. Comparing this baseline against competitor profiles reveals five high-value data fields and capabilities present on competing platforms that are missing from DataLabs' current profile snapshot.

\[Sector\] \[Founding Year\] \[HQ City\] \[Founders\] \[Total Funding\]  
                       │  
                       ▼  GAP ANALYSIS vs. COMPETITOR PLATFORMS  
1\. Audited MCA Financial Statements & Multi-Year Financial Ratios  
2\. Post-Deal Cap Tables & Shareholding Breakdown  
3\. Round-by-Round Valuations & Valuation Multiples  
4\. Investor Exit Analytics (Return Multiples / MoIC & Fund IRR)  
5\. Index of Charges & Secured Debt Capital Structure

### **1\. Audited MCA Financial Statements and Multi-Year Financial Ratios**

* **Competitor Presence**: Tracxn2, VCCEdge14, Venture Intelligence32.  
* **Value Rationale**: While total funding raised indicates historical investor backing, it does not reveal operational performance. Sourcing audited Income Statements, Balance Sheets, and Cash Flow Statements from MCA filings allows users to analyze revenue, EBITDA, expenses, and burn rates. Incorporating multi-year financial ratios (such as Current Ratio, Debt-to-Equity, and CAGR) gives institutional investors the necessary metrics to evaluate capital efficiency and solvency.

### **2\. Post-Deal Cap Tables and Shareholding Breakdown**

* **Competitor Presence**: Tracxn2, Venture Intelligence27, VCCEdge18.  
* **Value Rationale**: Understanding equity distribution among founders, ESOP pools, angel investors, and institutional VC funds is essential for deal underwriting. Detailed cap tables enable investors to evaluate founder dilution across funding rounds, identify secondary share sale opportunities, assess voting control, and structure liquidity terms during M\&A or follow-on investments.

### **3\. Round-by-Round Valuations and Valuation Multiples**

* **Competitor Presence**: Tracxn2, Venture Intelligence30, VCCEdge20.  
* **Value Rationale**: Aggregate funding totals omit pricing context. Displaying explicit Pre-Money Valuation, Post-Money Valuation, Enterprise Valuation (EV), and implied valuation multiples (EV/Revenue, EV/EBITDA) across each funding round allows analysts to benchmark pricing trends, evaluate down-round risks, and track valuation progression over time.

### **4\. Investor Exit Analytics (Return Multiples / MoIC and Fund IRR)**

* **Competitor Presence**: Venture Intelligence30, VCCEdge20, Tracxn9.  
* **Value Rationale**: Investors evaluate market opportunities through the lens of potential liquidity. Tracking M\&A, IPOs, and secondary exits alongside realized performance metrics—specifically Multiple on Invested Capital (MoIC / Cash-on-Cash multiples) and realized fund IRR—provides Limited Partners, General Partners, and investment bankers with the data required to evaluate fund performance and exit environments.

### **5\. Regulatory Index of Charges and Secured Debt Capital Structure**

* **Competitor Presence**: Venture Intelligence32, VCCEdge18.  
* **Value Rationale**: Equity financing represents only a portion of a company's total capital structure. Integrating MCA "Index of Charges" data—which documents registered bank loans, venture debt issuances, working capital lines, pledged assets, and open obligations—gives credit funds, debt investors, and M\&A advisors visibility into an entity's complete liability and leverage profile.

## **Confidence and Gaps**

This report relies on official product documentation, public preview pages, terms of service agreements, press statements, software review databases, and academic library portal indexes2. Below are the specific operational fields and pricing tiers that could not be independently verified due to platform paywalls, quote-based sales models, or conflicting sources:

* **Tracxn**: Official 2026 documentation confirms that all commercial subscriptions operate on an on-demand, quote-based model ("Contact Sales")2. Third-party aggregators list fixed historic packages (e.g., $550 per month for Single User, $1,100 per month for Small Team)6. These reflect cached baseline references rather than binding quotes. Deep profile fields (such as line-item balance sheets and cap tables) were verified via official platform feature documentation2, but cell-level verification on specific unlisted profiles remains gated behind Tracxn Lite monthly access caps2.  
* **VCCEdge / VCCircle**: VCCEdge publicly lists a standard domestic plan starting at ₹2,75,000 per year18, but multi-seat corporate packages, international accounts, and API add-ons require direct sales consultations18. While VCCEdge documents coverage of MCA filings, financial ratios, and cap table structures14, secondary digital tracking metrics (such as real-time website traffic signals or Glassdoor rating scores) were not observed in product materials and are considered unavailable.  
* **Venture Intelligence**: Venture Intelligence does not publish standard list prices for its institutional PE-VC Deals and Company Financials databases on its portal30. Pricing is publicly stated only for a la carte services (such as Competitive Intelligence summaries at ₹5,000 for 3 companies)33. Institutional database subscriptions require direct sales engagement31. Proprietary fields (including Index of Charges and Post-Deal Cap Tables) are confirmed in platform documentation30, but secondary operational signals (such as hiring trends, web analytics, or pitch deck repositories) were not verified on Venture Intelligence profile layouts.
