In-depth publishes in two blocks a day, separated by a four-hour hole

Plotting all 24 in-depth go-lives on a 24-hour clock, they do not spread across the day. Twelve land before 12:30, twelve land after 16:25, and nothing at all lands in between. The morning twelve arrive on round clock slots. The evening twelve arrive at ragged minutes — the moment the piece was finished.

The 24 in-depth pieces fall into three tight windows. The gap between the second and third is 3 hours 55 minutes — the entire post-lunch stretch of the working day, with no in-depth go-live in it once in 15 days.

The afternoon is unclaimed

Almost four hours of the working day, every day, with no in-depth go-live. Any afternoon push, digest or app refresh in that window has nothing new to carry.

1 of 12

The evening block is a queue, not a schedule

Eleven of the twelve evening pieces land on a ragged minute — they went live when the work cleared. Half of all in-depth output is effectively unscheduled.

50%

The most-worked format lands at the lowest-attention hour

Half of in-depth goes live after 16:25, and four pieces after 20:00 — one at 23:59. Moving even a share of that queue into the empty afternoon costs no extra output.

Sun → Mon

Monday morning is the thinnest supply point in the week

Sunday produces zero news and 1–2 morning in-depth pieces. Combined with 80% of news going live after 11:00, a Monday-morning surface is working almost entirely off Saturday.
