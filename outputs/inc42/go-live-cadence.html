Five patterns

0

Sunday is a pure in-depth day

Both Sundays produced zero news and only in-depth (1 and 2 pieces). Saturday halves to 7–9 news. The weekend is not a slow version of the week — it is a different product.

1.6

In-depth output is a constant, not a schedule

Averaging 1.6 pieces a day across all seven weekdays with no weekend dip. It behaves like a fixed team capacity draining at its own rate, independent of the news cycle.

42%

Depth arrives in bursts

Ten of the 24 in-depth pieces (42%) landed on just three days — 21 Aug, 29 Aug and 1 Sep. Daily counts swing 0 to 4. Sat 22 Aug was the only day in 15 with no in-depth at all.

11:00

One real slot exists; everything else is publish-when-ready

Six in-depth pieces went live at 11:00–11:19, the only time that repeats. The Monday 06:00 pair (Shiprocket, Purple Style Labs) is the second-most deliberate. The remaining 16 land at scattered times.

8%

The morning shelf is nearly empty

Only 8% of news and 8% of in-depth are live before 07:00. Anything assembled early in the morning is working almost entirely from the previous evening's batch — and on Monday, from Saturday's.

What follows from this

→

Monday morning is the thinnest supply point in the week

Sunday contributes 0 news and 1–2 in-depth. A morning surface built on the last 24 hours has the least to draw on precisely when the week's audience returns.

→

The highest-effort content goes live at the lowest-attention hour

42% of in-depth publishes after 17:00. That is the format with the most work behind it landing when the day is closing.

→

Weekend inventory is in-depth or nothing

Across four weekend days: 16 news items total, 12 of them on Saturday. Any Saturday or Sunday experience is carried by 1–3 long reads.

Window: 19 Aug – 2 Sep 2026, 15 calendar days, 209 go-lives (179 news, 24 in-depth, 5 startups, 1 resources). All times IST. Weekday averages rest on 2–3 observations each, so treat the day-of-week table as direction, not a settled baseline. Percentages are rounded.
