The north-star number — and why it's soft

verify before quoting
What Utkarsh stated
~2,100qualified today
  • Derived: 21,298 ninety-day identified actives × 9.99% with role and resolved company ≈ 2,128.
  • 43.2% of the 21,298 have a role; only 9.99% also have a resolved company.
Why it's a ceiling, not the number
  • Wrong window. QIA is a 30-day metric; 2,100 uses the 90-day base.
  • Strict floor. "Role + resolved company" is narrower than "known role, in core market" (role-only ≈ 9,200).
  • Un-measurable today. 3 PostHog projects + 2 Customer.io workspaces can't see one person.
Verdict
True QIA-30 ≈ 1,200–1,500, not 2,100 — and it can't be measured cross-surface until the identity spine exists. Pull the real QIA-30 from BigQuery before setting any target.
