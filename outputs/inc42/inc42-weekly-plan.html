Weekly plan — validate the number, fix the instruments, then move the funnel

The engine has one scoreboard (QIA) and one funnel beneath it: acquisition → activation → engagement. Every experiment names the funnel stage it moves and how that rolls up to the number. But nothing is read until the number itself is trustworthy — so validating QIA-30 is the gate on everything else.

⛔
The gate — validate before we do anything. The starting number (~2,100) is a 90-day, company-resolved estimate that can't be measured cross-surface today. Week 1 pulls the real QIA-30 from BigQuery and fixes the events that feed it. No Phase-2 experiment is read against a number until this clears.

Why it's a ceiling, not the number
  • Wrong window. QIA is a 30-day metric (revised 12 Aug). 2,100 uses the 90-day base — the strategy itself flags "the 30-day baseline must be pulled."
  • Strict floor. "Role + resolved company" is narrower than QIA's "known role, in core market." Role-only reads ~9,200.
  • Un-measurable today. 3 PostHog projects + 2 Customer.io workspaces can't see one person; no enrichment layer.

Verdict
Apply the 30-day haircut and the true QIA-30 is roughly 1,200–1,500 — not 2,100 — and even that isn't measurable correctly until the identity spine exists.

So the plan does not chase a QIA target yet. Week 1 validates the number. Everything downstream is read against a QIA-30 we can actually trust.

2

The funnel — acquisition → activation → engagement

activation is defined per surface
Stage 1

Acquisition

A new person reaches a surface — from search, social, a newsletter, or an app install.

Weblands on an article / DataLabs page
Appinstalls & opens
Emailnewsletter click → surface
Measure: new visitors · installs · attributable source
Stage 2

Activation

The first real value action — defined by the surface — plus the identity capture that makes the person known.

Editorialreads an article (to depth)
DataLabsbrowses a company profile
Appcompletes a brief (≥60s)
Identityregisters → role + employer asked
Makes: Identified (register) + Qualified (role+company)
Stage 3

Engagement

The person comes back and takes a qualifying action inside a rolling 30 days. This is the "active" in QIA.

Webreturns → 2nd+ article / profile
DataLabssearch · alert · saved search · Ask
Appreturns → completes another brief
Measure: 30-day qualifying action · repeat-day rate
Rolls up to the north star: Identified∩ Qualified∩ Active · 30d= QIA
