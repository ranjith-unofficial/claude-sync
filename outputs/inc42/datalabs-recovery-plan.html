Winning back Datalabs subscribers

Seven problems, what to do about each, and the experiment that tells us whether it worked. Roughly half of what we lose is recoverable — and almost none of it needs persuasion.

01 — CONTEXT

What you need to know first

WhenWhat happensAmountHealth
Day 0 7-day trial starts. Customer sets up a mandate — standing permission to debit them later. Razorpay proves it works with a ₹10 charge. ₹10 FINE 90.8%
Day 7 First real charge. The moment a trial becomes a paying customer. ₹1,768.82 57.9% was 73.7% in May
Day 37+ Monthly renewals. Monthly is the only plan — no annual, no quarterly, no seats. ₹1,768.82 16 subs ever reached a 2nd charge

57.9% is a success rate, not an attempt rate. Every trial with a mandate gets an automatic Day-7 charge attempt — nobody is skipped. Reconciled from Section 02's failure count: ~95 attempts, 55 succeeded (57.9%), 40 failed for the reasons broken down there. This isn't stated directly in the source data — it's derived by matching the two numbers, so treat it as high-confidence, not confirmed.

The pre-debit notification. NPCI requires the customer to be notified 24 hours before every automatic debit. We can't switch it off or change what it says.

GPay · 24 hours before Inc42 Media will debit ₹1,768.82 on 14 Sep.

Tapping it opens the mandate screen, which carries pause and revoke. So the reminder and the exit arrive together — a cold price with no context, twelve times a year. Nationally this is where ~20 million mandates a month get revoked.

Two words used below. Revoked = customer killed the mandate; it's dead, a new one is needed. Paused = suspended but still alive; only they can resume it.

03 — PROBLEMS & SOLUTIONS

Seven problems

Ordered by what they're worth. P1 and P2's headline numbers add to 19 of the 21 projected recoveries (see the reconciliation note in Section 02).

Problem Evidence Fix Experiment bar Worth
P1A failed payment is never tried again 47 failed charges since launch, zero retries — while our emails say "we'll retry automatically." Insufficient balance is 45% of failures; UPI debits once, no second look.
  • Check whether retry is simply switched off — WooCommerce Subscriptions ships it disabled by default
  • Retry only balance + bank-declined; never an unconfirmed/cancelled/inactive mandate
  • Time to payday: Day 7 → +3d → +7d → 1st–3rd of month; cap 4 attempts / 15 days
  • Log every attempt (number, time, code, outcome) — today 111 of 113 cycles log exactly one
 ≥50% of insufficient-balance failures recover within 15 days. Baseline 0%. ~11
P2Trials start on mandates that were never confirmed 30% of first-charge failures. All 14 were UPI — the ₹10 captured 5–6 days before the real charge failed. Trial marked active before the mandate was proven.
  • Don't mark trial active until mandate confirms — capture ≠ confirmation on UPI
  • Prompt in-session if unconfirmed within minutes
  • Auto-resend the mandate request
  • Daily sweep for active subs without a confirmed mandate — 9 paying + 5 of 25 trials affected right now
 Unconfirmed mandates drop below 10% of failures, from 30%. Bug fix, not a campaign. ~8
P3When a mandate dies, we have no way to tell them 73 subscriptions revoked right now, no failed charge yet, none contacted. User Type has no failure state. A banner alone won't reach the drifted — 30 of 31 payers logged in last month, but that's payers, not this cohort.
  • Add a payment-failure value to User Type — prerequisite for everything below
  • Day 0: banner, WhatsApp, and email fire together, not staggered — a small cohort, no reason to wait
  • In-product banner, 4 states (never confirmed / revoked / payment failed / paused)
  • WhatsApp utility template (₹0.115/msg, fact + link only, no offer)
  • Email too — weakest channel historically (CIO lane got 0.2% vs its 0.8% bar, killed) but free to include
  • Give each channel its own tracked link, so the per-channel bars below still read cleanly even though they launch together
  • High value: a person writes directly to the 9 detached payers (~₹15,919/mo)
 Measure reach split first (% opening Datalabs within 7 days of mandate death). Bars: banner ≥3%, WhatsApp ≥3%, email beats 0.2%, combined ≥8%. ~1
P4We find out too late to do anything Revocations surface via periodic poll, not the webhook. In 0 of 91 cases was the revocation recorded before the next charge had already bounced.
  • Wire the token.cancelled webhook for same-day recording
  • Keep polling only as a backstop
  • Alert on any active sub with no next charge date (the silent-expiry case from P2)
  • Read all 3 note sources — subscription notes alone miss 110 cancellations (93 + 17 sit on order records)
 100% of revocations recorded before any charge is attempted (currently 0 of 91). Precondition for P3, not really a test. —
P5A paused mandate gets treated like a dead one 263 logged "paused" instances, note reads "Monitoring," nothing fires. Pause is the opposite of cancel — roughly 3 in 4 pausers come back.
  • Suppress all dunning to paused customers, today
  • Branch messaging on pause_initiated_by
  • One nudge only, on the resume date — nothing before it
  • Offer pause ourselves: membership page + first offer in any cancel flow (Cult.fit-style)
 ≥15% of cancel-starters choose pause instead. First pull the distinct count — 263 is note occurrences, not people. —
P6Twelve cancel prompts a year, and we're absent for all of them Monthly-only plan = 12 pre-debit notifications/year, no context, next to an exit. We don't even log when they fire.
  • Log the notification timestamp — unlocks: do cancellations spike in the 72h after one?
  • Land a value message 6–12h before each notification
  • Day-5 trial message before the day-6 notification (₹10 consented vs ₹1,768.82 charged — 177×)
  • DECISION Longer billing cycle — quarterly cuts 12 prompts to 4, ₹5,306 vs ₹17,626
 ≥20% fewer cancellations in the 72h window after a notification. If quarterly ships: ≥10% of new trials choose it. —
P7Four groups nobody has ever contacted All live today, all identifiable, none written to: revoked-not-yet-broken (73, still have access), paused (TBC, best prospects), paying-mandate-detached (9, ~₹15,919/mo, will expire silently), trials that can't convert (5 of 25, no working mandate).
  • A person sends these, not a system — an afternoon's work; the automated lane already failed here once
  • Recognise, don't pitch — one-question opener: "Your auto-pay for Datalabs is off. Access runs to {date}. Was that deliberate?"
  • Contact the 73 before their charge dates, not after
  • Log every reply verbatim — that transcript is the segmentation
 ≥20% reply rate, enough reasons recorded to write the automated version. —
