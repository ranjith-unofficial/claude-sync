! The one target that isn't incremental

Activation goes up five points on a base that already works. Web acquisition roughly doubles off a very small number. D7 has to triple. 15% is an ordinary figure for a daily-news app — it is the jump that is hard, not the destination.

What has to be true for D7 to move
  • Push has to actually fire. It never has. The payload is silent, and a silent push is never delivered once the app is force-killed — the fix is an APS alert payload. This is likely the single biggest D7 lever on the list, and it is a bug fix, not a feature.
  • Read stories must never be re-served. Reopen at story six. Same content on every open trains people down to once a day, then to zero.
  • Relevance has to come from behaviour. Sector personalisation is dead — 86% of articles carry no sector — so read behaviour and dwell are the only signals left.
  • Deep links have to work, or the newsletter cannot pull anyone back into the app at all.

Three of those four sit outside the September build: they are August's bug fixes. If they do not land, 15% is not reachable by anything done inside the month.

Recommendation: carry it the way Utkarsh asked for every goal — practical 8–10%, ambitious 15%. A single 3× number with no floor under it reads as a miss in October even if D7 doubles.
