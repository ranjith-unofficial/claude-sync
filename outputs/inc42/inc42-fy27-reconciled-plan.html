One INC42 · Product Plan

The product plan, by funnel stage

Scoped to the tracks Ranjith owns or is on — nothing from brand, social, video, editorial or IPs. It follows one person through the funnel: how they arrive, get known, find value, form a habit, stay, and pay.

Aligned to Utkarsh's locked FY27 plan (21 Aug 2026). Numbers are directional until the north star becomes measurable ~20 Sep.

The funnel, in one look

Seven stages. Product owns all of them except Awareness, which sits with Distribution.

  • Awareness — being found as one brand.Owned by Distribution (Nityam) — out of this product plan.
  • Acquisition — people arrive, and we learn who they are at the door.This is the leak: only 0.16–0.42% of visitors are identified today.
  • Activation — a known person gets real value, defined per surface.
  • Engagement — they form a habit and reach a second surface.The mechanism competition runs here — this is the heart of the plan.
  • Retention — they're still here months later, and we've asked them to stay.
  • Revenue — we learn what they'll pay for, then charge for it.
  • Referral — they bring others in.

What we focus on, month by month

One or two stages per month — not everything at once.

Month 0
Foundation. Fix the plumbing — measurement, the identity spine, event fixes, feature flags. Nothing above can be measured until this lands.
Month 1
Acquisition. Start knowing who arrives — capture at app sign-in, run the web gate test, one onboarding pattern.
Month 2
Activation. Make the reading path lead somewhere — cross-linking, entity cards, unified search, AskInc42 — and name the habit candidates.
Month 3
Engagement. Give people reasons to come back — push, alerts, community, the Weekly. The mechanism competition ramps.
Month 4
Engagement & Retention. Score the winners; run reactivation and renewal.
Month 5
Retention & Revenue. Read the price probe; shape the membership and pricing.

The few numbers that matter

The whole plan turns on one problem: we deliver value at scale, then identify almost no one.

Of ~478,000 website visitors in a month, only 1,986 (0.42%) become known to us. On DataLabs, 211 of ~136,000 (0.16%). That is why Acquisition is the priority — traffic was never the constraint; identity is.

QIA (the north star) — Utkarsh's stated figure is ~2,100 qualified people (30-day, across web + DataLabs + email + app). It's computed on only 3 of its 9 activities today, so it will jump up when the rest are piped in (~20 Sep) — that's a measurement fix, not growth.

Activation on DataLabs — ~197 people a week do 2+ searches (WAAU). This is weekly, and is not the same as the 30-day QIA.

Paying — 83 active paying subscriptions today. (Don't confuse with the 278 payment-event count or the 1,045 lifetime subscriber base — three different definitions.)
