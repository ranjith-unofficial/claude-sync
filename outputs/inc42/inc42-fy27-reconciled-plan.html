What we focus on, month by month

Month 0
Foundation. Fix the plumbing — measurement, the identity spine, event fixes, feature flags. Nothing above can be scored until this lands.
Month 1
Awareness + Acquisition. Be found as one brand (llm.txt, Google Discover) and start knowing who arrives (sign-in capture, the gate test).
Month 2
Activation + Engagement. Make the reading path lead somewhere (cross-linking, entity cards, unified search) and name & scope the habit candidates.
Month 3
Engagement. Reasons to come back — the mechanism competition ramps (alerts, community, Datalabs Weekly, push).
Month 4
Engagement + Retention. Deepen and hold — score the winners, run reactivation and renewal.
Month 5
Retention + Revenue. First revenue read — the price-probe result, unified pricing, and the shape of the membership.
