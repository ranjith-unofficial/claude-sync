Brand read

How colour works on inc42.com/datalabs

Read from the live site on 15 Sep: its stylesheets and page screens. The site is mostly black on white. Red appears in small, deliberate amounts: one phrase, one button, one bar per section.

Fonts: the site uses Geist (body and headings) and Geist Mono (labels). Apple Mail and iOS Mail can load Geist. Gmail and Outlook cannot, and fall back to Helvetica or Arial. The mockups below show Geist; every layout also holds in Arial.

Recommendation

Launch with 3 · Money Map. Swap its hero for 4 · Your Sector Week for any reader with a sector set.

  • The Money Map is the only shape with one strong visual, no blocked dependency, and no reliance on filings or stock moves showing up that week.
  • Variant 4’s sector picker sits in every issue’s footer from day one. It fills sector on the profile, which unlocks the personal hero.
  • Use the Scoreboard’s win / lose board as the “Off the map” block when a week has 2 or more filing or stock pairs.
  • Hold 5 · Signal Feed until “Add to list” lands signed in. Without it, the cards are a list with dead buttons.
  • Skip 1 as the lead. It fixes the brand but keeps today’s report feel.
