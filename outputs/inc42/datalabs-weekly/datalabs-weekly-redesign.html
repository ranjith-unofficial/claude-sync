Datalabs Weekly Redesign

Review of the automated issue (content preview, pipeline logic, and the 20 Aug test email as it lands in Gmail), plus the proposed structure, recall mechanics, and channel plan.

1 · Verdict

The engine is right. The last mile is not.

Logic: keepSQL computes, AI only writes. Freshness gate, market fail-close, claim rule, no empty sections. This is better than most newsletters.
Numbers: 2 bugs reach the inboxSector values do not add up to the total. Prior-week comparison looks broken.
Layout: long, flat, 9 CTAsEvery section gets equal weight and its own red button. Nothing tells the reader what matters.
Product: clicks leave DatalabsCompany names open inc42.com articles. Nothing puts a company on a watchlist or keeps the reader logged in.
Personalisation: built, but offNo sector on the Customer.io profile and no icp, so everyone gets the generic issue.
