Four in ten articles ship with no sector

The 337, decomposed

Sector-missing articles

Splitting the sector gap by what else the row already contains changes the diagnosis completely. Only 155 of 337 are genuinely untagged content. The rest are rows where the answer is sitting one column over.

137 — resolvable from the same row

The row already carries a valid industry path. 99 can be copied verbatim; 38 need a one-hop roll-up from a sub-industry to its parent. Zero editorial judgement required.

42 — company known, never sector-tagged

Named companies with no sector anywhere in the corpus. Fixable once, in the company master, then every past and future article inherits it.

158 — genuinely dark

113 have no company, no sector and no industry at all. Another 42 carry only an orphan taxonomy value. These need a human.

The finding: 178 articles have a populated primary_industry but a blank sector. Where both fields are filled, they agree 92.2% of the time (435 of 472). Sector is not being authored independently — it is a copy of industry that silently fails to write.

That reframes 178 of the 337 from an editorial-training problem into a one-time backfill plus a pipeline fix.

What to do

In order
1

Fix the write, not the rows

Sector agrees with primary industry 92.2% of the time and is blank on 178 rows where industry is populated. Find the step that derives sector and make it fire on every publish, including edits. Without this, any backfill re-decays at ~120 articles a month.

pipeline · 1 fix
2

Add Startup Ecosystem as a 17th sector

28 articles — trackers, round-ups, fund launches — plus 11 investor entities have a real, coherent industry value with no sector to map to. One taxonomy decision clears the largest single orphan bucket.

taxonomy · 1 decision
3

Run the 137-row automated backfill

99 rows copy their industry root verbatim; 38 need a one-hop parent roll-up. Deterministic, reversible, no editorial input. Tab 3 of the workbook is the exact row list with the suggested value and how it was derived.

137 rows · script
4

Resolve 31 orphan taxonomy values

Map each sub-industry to its parent, merge AgriTech into Agritech, and add a validation rule so primary_industry can only ever hold a top-level node. This is what stops leak #1 recurring.

31 values
5

Backfill 81 companies in the company master

Fix once at the company level and every past and future article inherits. 39 of the 81 already show a usable industry on their article. Resolve the Skyroot and Blinkit contradictions first.

81 companies
6

Give the 113 dark articles a fallback

Government & Policies (33), Business Updates (31) and Controversies (17) dominate. Most are about regulators, platforms or the ecosystem rather than a startup. Either allow a sector without a company, or accept them as untagged deliberately — but decide, rather than leaving them to fall through.

113 articles
7

Replace all four blank sentinels with real nulls

NULL, null, undefined, TBD and empty string all mean the same thing today. Until they don't, every downstream completeness check under-counts the gap and TBD reads as a company name in 108 rows.
