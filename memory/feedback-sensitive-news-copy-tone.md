Said 14 Sep 2026 after the Zomato layoff copy (240 jobs, Hyderabad support shut). Ranjith: *"layoff or any
government news ... are sensitive. So you cannot simply say a random thing ... I don't want you to be
empathetic also. But ... I don't want you to be funny about this as well."*

Approved example: "Why did Zomato shut its Hyderabad customer support team and cut 240 jobs?" /
"Who will handle Zomato's customer support after 240 layoffs in Hyderabad?"
Rejected: "What are the 240 employees getting?" (severance side detail, reads as a tease).

**Why:** a curiosity hook on bad news reads as mocking; a sympathetic one reads as editorialising. Inc42's voice
is a news desk.

**How to apply:** for layoffs, shutdowns, regulator/govt action, court cases, fraud allegations, deaths, breaches:
- only Why (stated reason) or Who/what now questions; no wrong-expectation, contradiction or how-big angles
- never about affected people's pay or situation
- no sympathy words, no slang ("axe", "pink slips"); neutral tap lines ("Tap to read why.")
- never import a cause from an earlier event (AI was cited for Zomato's 2025 layoffs, not this one)
