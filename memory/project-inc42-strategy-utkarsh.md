**The vision in one line:** "One Inc42: the intelligence engine for India's startup economy." One product, one identity, one membership — content+data+community+AI. Free for everyone building; known by name at the core; convened in rooms admission cannot buy.

**THE ENGINE (5 stages, each feeds the next, the last pays for the first):** Reach (millions, free journalism + social/video) → **Know** (tens of thousands — sign-up opens the database, ask who you are once) → **Deepen** (thousands — one membership) → **Convene** (hundreds — curated rooms/IPs) → **Pay** (the primary business — sponsors buy the room and, for the first time, PROOF of who's in it). Product owns stages 2–3, serves 4–5.

**NORTH STAR = QIA (Qualified Identified Actives)** — people with a known role, inside the core professional market, who took a qualifying action in a rolling **30-day** window (revised from 90). Targets (FY convention, horizon FY31): **15,000 (FY28) → 35–40,000 (FY30) → ~50,000 (FY31)**. Paired with **IA-30** (volume line: any identified person, ~30k→120k) and **R30** (anonymous reach: unique browsers reading 2+ pieces/30d, keeps the 99.71% logged-out majority visible). Retired the old **WEIU** metric as "blind to 99.71% of arrivals." Qualifying actions = any one, attributable to an identified person: content read with measured depth (Scroll Depth restored first) · profile/search/database view · watchlist/saved-search/alert · Ask query · app session · newsletter CLICK (never an open) · event application/registration · community contribution · payment/renewal. Never counted: email opens, push receipts, bare login, staff/internal (127 internal accounts once = 21% of identified pageviews), bots. **Honest starting position (2026-08-11): 21,298 90-day identified actives, 43.2% role-classifiable, 9.99% with role+company (~2,100 truly qualified today).**

**★ Registration is THE central mechanism — because there is NO enrichment layer.** A full warehouse scan for enrichment vendors returned nothing; every job title is self-reported on a form. So: **profiles + the database become free-but-registration-gated (articles stay OPEN, never walled)** — gating adds identity, never price. Every capture point asks role + employer type + timestamp. This is the "qualified-audience engine" that sponsors ultimately pay for.

**OPERATING MODEL (decided, not open):** Subscriptions/membership will NEVER be the primary revenue channel — they're **secondary, their primary purpose is engagement & retention**. The rule: "membership revenue is welcome to grow but never bought at the audience's expense — no feature ships to make the number, no engagement mechanism degraded to monetise; growth is a free win, never a chased target." Reading is never asked to monetise directly (reader→payer = 1.41%, a hard category norm, unbridged anywhere). Price maximises the NUMBER of qualified people in a paid relationship, not revenue per head (the asset is what the primary line — rooms — sells).

**THE TIERS (one ladder, one audience, fenced on DEPTH not audience):** Free ₹0 (identity — articles open, profiles/db registration-gated) · **T1 ₹4,999–14,999/yr** (core TAM; utility: alerts, saved searches, capped exports, Deal Digest, reports, community, uncapped Ask, **monthly digital sessions**) · **T2 ₹25,000–50,000/yr** (power users of T1 — SAME audience more depth: contacts, financials, cap tables, uncapped exports, analyst credits) · **T3 deferred** (invite-only capacity-capped rooms; ticketing is the funnel into it). **Griffin is completely off the ladder** (independent pinnacle). Selection is NEVER sold (verification→invitation→invoice, in that order). T2 is NOT a second audience — an audience split would recreate the exact fragmentation being fixed; the buyer segment EMERGES from who upgrades. FY28 feasibility: ₹1–2 Cr (Yr1: 800 T1 @₹7,500 + 100 T2 @₹37,500 ≈ ₹1 Cr) against 1,045 already-active subs to migrate.

**MECHANISM IS DELIBERATELY OPEN — no spine chosen in advance.** Outcome fixed (identify→activate→retain); the app, six live newsletters, community, follow-graph, entity card all COMPETE on QIA, and winners get investment. Standing instruction, not a preference. The three owed designs are mechanism *competitions*, not committed architectures: Identify = unified onboarding (role+sector once) · Activate = cross-surface discovery (in-body entity card, article→entity→membership) · Retain = lifecycle journeys, state-based not stage-based (Datalabs Weekly, alerts-to-build, community, the Brief, the rooms + between-rooms community = the belonging loop).

**HYPOTHESIS LEDGER (A1–A13, each with a pre-registered bar) — the roadmap is an ordered sequence of hypotheses, NOT a feature list.** Engine (validate first): A2 registration-gating profiles doesn't destroy feeding traffic (holdout) · A3 asking role+employer lifts qualification · A12 retain mechanisms lift 30-day activity (the mechanism competitions ARE the test) · A13 unified cross-surface experience + one brand message lift cross-surface usage (today 1.6–3.4%). Probe: A1 legacy subscribers migrate (a willingness PROBE, ~20% pooled bar — START NOW, needs nothing built, decides FY28's shape). Offering (FY28): A4 unified membership retains where content-only & data-only failed · A8 T2 fills with power users not a 2nd product · A9 members renew (no comparable publishes a renewal rate). Room's value (H2): A5 composition measurable/warrantable · A6 warranted composition raises room worth · A7 room buyers ↔ membership buyers overlap. Org: A10 org can run a recurring-revenue motion (evidenced against — a 1-char fix unshipped 23 months) · A11 AI-native ops holds cost base flat while QIA scales 4×.

**EXPLICITLY NOT BUILDING (§11):** a data-completeness race (leader runs negative EBITDA) · a content paywall in any form (articles stay open; the freewall is REMOVED, "Wave 0") · a second product line for the demand side · a shared follow-graph as the committed spine (doesn't exist; mechanisms compete) · a combined app interface as the strategy (the app is a PILOT — few hundred users, no store listing, retention edge INVERTS once the hand-recruited cohort is removed) · a Datalabs enterprise sales motion (0 renewals from 17 expired terms) · rooms inside T2 · paid reactivation of the dormant base.
