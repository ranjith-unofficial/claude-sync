Aug 2026. Ranjith is building a **dunning + win-back system for Datalabs Pro subscriptions** (Razorpay recurring, UPI AutoPay). Source docs: Notion page *"Subscription Dunning, Recovery & Winback System"* (`3b85d94315658114ae17f1653876455d`, the engineering spec — plumbing complete, Customer.io Phase 4 blocked, REST auth hardening required before prod) and **`~/Downloads/Funnel State 2026 07.md`** (the diagnostic, 2026-07-30, PostHog + Razorpay + Metorik + WooCommerce REST). Deliverables: `~/ClaudeDocs/inc42/datalabs-recovery-plan.html` → artifact **efaafcd9-c00a-464d-92d8-1e30e08e5702** + `datalabs-recovery-tracker.csv`. **The plan file has been edited from another session — re-read before editing.**

**THE SCALE. Do not plan as if this is a big business.** 48 lifetime payers · **31 active subs** · ₹54,833 gross MRR · ₹94,437 net collected since Apr 2026 launch · net LTV/payer ≈ ₹2,317 · 37 of 48 payers have exactly one successful charge. **LTV lost to failed first debits ≈ ₹92,665 — the leak has cost roughly what the business has earned.** (The "1,045 subs" in Utkarsh's strategy doc is all of Inc42+, NOT Datalabs Pro — I used it and was off ~20×.)

**THE MECHANICS.** Day 0 = 7-day trial, ₹10 mandate authorisation (90.8% succeed). **Day 7 = first real charge ₹1,499+18% GST = ₹1,768.82.** Day 37+ = monthly renewals. **Monthly is the ONLY self-serve plan** — no annual, no quarterly, no seats (PostHog's `Pro Membership Type` still shows legacy "Quarterly"/"Yearly" values, 2 and 1 in 90d — not purchasable, don't misread them).

**THE CORE FAILURE: 47 failed recurring charges since launch, ZERO retries, ever.** One failed debit is terminal 100% of the time — while live transactional copy promises *"we'll retry automatically"* and cites *"2 retries over 48h"*. That flow does not exist. **WooCommerce Subscriptions ships retry OFF by default — check the setting before scoping a build.** First-charge capture **57.9% and degrading** (May 73.7% → Jun 54.2% → Jul 53.8%).

**Two numbers that get confused:** **57.9%** = of charges *attempted*, share where money was actually *captured* (95 subs reached it, 55 captured; attempts = subs because there are no retries). **25.4%** = trial-to-paid. The gap: only **50.3% of mature trials ever reach a day-7 attempt at all**. The funnel doc's own heading ("the day-7 trial-to-paid conversion") is what causes the confusion.

**Failure mix at first debit:** insufficient balance 45% · **`RECURRING_TOKEN_NOT_CONFIRMED` 30% (a BUG — all 14 were UPI and all had ₹10 captured 5–6 days earlier; we took money and granted access against a mandate that never existed; still firing 21 Jul)** · mandate inactive/cancelled 10% · bank declined 5%. **Silent expiry bug:** after a first charge succeeds `requires_manual_renewal` flips true + `next_payment_date` goes null → subscription expires with NO debit attempted (100% correlation across all 27 expired trials + all 5 expired payers). **9 live subs (~₹15,919/mo) and 5 of 25 current trials are detached right now.**

**CHURN SPLIT (customer-level, 346 sub records → 270 people):** started a real trial then ended it **135 (102 voluntary, 75.6%)** · **paying customers lost only 12 (8 involuntary, 66.7%)** · never started 58 (not churn). **The character flips at the first successful payment** — before it, churn is a choice; after it, it's mechanics. **Win-back of paying churners addresses 12 people. The volume is upstream.**

**MANDATE DEATHS.** `token.cancelled` = 91 external cancels (UPI app) vs **33 cancel-at-period-end via our own UI** (so the split is ~73/27, NOT 100/0 — I wrongly claimed our surface was structurally unreachable). **73 subs revoked with no failed charge yet** = the only cohort where we're ahead of the problem. **263 "token paused / Monitoring" notes — nothing fires** (note occurrences from a repeating poll, get the distinct count). **Detection is by polling, not the webhook: in 0 of 91 cases did we record the revocation before the charge already failed.** Also: 93 `customer cancelled` notes sit on parent orders + 17 on renewal orders with no counterpart on the subscription record — reading subscription notes alone misses 110 cancellations.

**LIVE POSTHOG FINDINGS (project 66351, pulled 23 Aug):**
- **`User Type` already segments Free 2,849 / Pro Trial 43 / Pro Member 30 / Plus Member 17** (30d logins) — **but has NO failure state.** Adding one value unlocks every in-product dunning banner. This is the cheapest high-leverage task in the plan.
- **30 of 31 payers logged in within 30 days** → renewal failure is mechanical, not disengagement.
- **ZERO trialists did nothing**; 75% took 26+ actions during the trial. Killed my "don't chase low-value trials" segment — it described 3.5% of people.
- Checkout funnel 30d (people): Upgrade clicked **225** → Checkout started **122** → Payment initiated **38**. Checkout *errors* are rare (5 in 90d) — people abandon, they don't hit errors.
- **Payment events are untrustworthy:** `Payment Completed` fired ZERO times mid-Jun→13 Jul; `Payment Completed_Backend` on "Free Trial" = the ₹10 auth (291 people/90d), not revenue. Money only from Razorpay/Metorik.
- **`Alert Interaction` fires 1,831× for 1,815 people** — a page-load state readout, not an action; its retention "lift" is spurious.
- **Session replay, surveys and heatmaps are all OFF in 66351.** Free, no engineering, and we currently cannot watch a failed checkout or ask a churning customer why.
- **`Register Lock Interaction` is alive again** (~1,000/wk, 3,364 people/30d) — the funnel doc called it dead since 18 May.

**IDEAS THAT LOOKED GOOD AND ARE DEAD (don't re-propose):** rewriting the export-limit modal (**`Limit Reached` fired 8× for 3 people in 60 days, all on the AI query cap**; only 40 people exported anything in 30d; heaviest used 42 of 100 credits, median 2–6) · **credit packs as a win-back offer** (zero purchases since 22 Apr, 3 of 4 packs have no purchasable SKU, `Credit Payment` has no completion stage) · annual-as-"twice-the-credits" (credits aren't the constraint, and no annual plan exists) · export-button value counters (2.6% adoption) · **any automated win-back campaign** (Customer.io reactivation already ran: 1,528 visitors → **3 logins, 0.2%**, against a pre-set 0.8% kill threshold; dark since 6 Jul. The one send that worked was MoEngage at 37.8% login rate, never repeated).

**THE UPI STRUCTURAL PROBLEM.** NPCI **requires** a pre-debit notification 24h before *every* debit; it names Inc42 + the amount and opens a screen carrying pause and revoke. So regulation forces a monthly re-decision we can't switch off — **12 a year on monthly, 1 on annual.** Nationally ~20M mandates/month get revoked, largely after failed debits. **We don't log when the PDN fires** → can't yet test whether cancels cluster in the 72h after it (the single most diagnostic question here). Also unconfirmed: whether Razorpay auto-fires the PDN on a retry or we must — it sets the minimum retry interval (~48h/attempt).

**THE PLAN'S SHAPE — 7 problems, each with numbered solutions + a pre-registered bar.** P1 retries (worth ~11 of 21 recoveries) · P2 catch unconfirmed mandates in minutes not day 7 (~8) · P3 **reach cascade** (in-product banner day 0 → **WhatsApp +48h if they don't log in — we already collect Phone Number at checkout, utility templates permit failed-payment notices at ₹0.115, no offers allowed in them** → email +5d → a human for high value) · P4 webhook not polling · P5 treat paused ≠ dead (suppress dunning immediately; offer pause ourselves, Cult.fit placement not Netflix's) · P6 the 12 PDNs (log the timestamp; value message before it; **build a longer cycle — quarterly cuts 12 prompts to 4 at ₹5,306 vs ₹17,626 — this is a PRICING DECISION, not a build**) · P7 the four uncontacted lists. **Target: ~52% of failed charges recoverable, and the two biggest rows are bugs, not persuasion.**

**EXPLICITLY OUT OF SCOPE but too big to lose:** **513 people clicked upgrade on `Datalabs Pro Contacts - Monthly` in 90 days and NOT ONE reached checkout** (largest intent pool in the funnel, dead-ends completely) · **activation = 2 advanced filters in week 1** (return 10.4% at zero → 22.7% at one → **41.9% at 2–3** → 48.5% at 10+; only **11.3%** ever use one) · people with 2+ filters convert paywall→trial at **34.2% vs 13.5%** but **229 of 349 never saw a paywall at all** · mobile = a third of registrations converting at ~a ninth the rate (793 registered → 35 filter users → 9 trials).

**How to apply:** treat this as a **payments/plumbing problem first and a messaging problem second** — the plan's own sequencing is fix-the-bucket-then-refill. Never size it off Inc42+ numbers. Never propose an automated campaign before a human has proven a message on the ~73-person list.
