On 14 Sep 2026 Ranjith flagged "Plus 5 more stories picked for you" under three question sheets on the Brief card:
*"first three are question … if we simply write 5 more stories which is misleading … it should follow the flow."*

**Why:** the reader has just been shown three *questions*; switching to "stories" in the continuation line breaks the
sentence the card is building and makes the count feel like a different kind of thing. (Earlier, 14 Sep, he had
rejected "answers" as the noun because what is inside is articles — the fix is not to go back to "answers", it is to
continue the noun already on screen.)

**How to apply:** any "N more …" / "and N more …" / "X left" line that follows a list must reuse that list's noun
("Plus 5 more questions picked for you", "Two questions left"). Lines that state the brief's total size on their own
("8 stories picked for you") are separate and can say stories. Applied across rounds 7–8 on draft page 736:2
(22 card lines + notes). Related: [[project-inc42-brief-card-questions-round]], [[feedback-design-review-ranjith]].
