**Key findings, worth reusing directly:**
- **The "four games" framing**: every operator sorts into one of 4 boxes — *Claims* authority (states scale/seniority as the pitch, no proof), *Shows* it (proves live — a demo, working pricing ladder, cross-linking), *Funded elsewhere* (free, ads/sponsorship/founder-funded), or *Subscription purist* (payment itself is the pitch, e.g. The Ken). **INC42 is the one brand split across two boxes at once** — the main site *claims* ("India's #1... 10M readers") while the only place we actually *show* the capability (sharp, data-backed writing) is DataLabs, sealed off from the brand making the claim. This independently confirms [[project-inc42-unification]]'s core thesis (backend/capability exists, isn't exposed) via a completely different method — worth citing whenever that thesis needs a second, non-analytics form of evidence.
- **Feature-matrix highlights relevant to unification**: INC42 = ❌ unified login, ❌ unified pricing, ⚠️ limited cross-linking — confirmed from the outside, matching the internal PostHog/GA4 read. Tracxn and Tech in Asia are the two "Yes" rows on unified login+pricing (Tracxn because it's a single product with no editorial arm at all; Tech in Asia because it deliberately built one ladder spanning news+data). Crunchbase has the strongest cross-linking of the set.
- **entrackr, read directly**: no consistent tagline, "aspirational boilerplate only" positioning copy, fully free/ad-supported, publicly names Paytm's and Razorpay's founders as investors — voice described as "fast, dense, scrappy, terminal-like." Read as a hypothesis (**access/trust moat from founder-investor ties, not necessarily product polish**) for why it holds engagement despite less traffic than INC42 — not yet confirmed by a real product audit, see [[project-inc42-unification]] v14/v15's Phase 3.
- **The Arc** (VC-backed, not a direct competitor) is the standing template for turning a static company profile into a "living page": hard data card + named analyst commentary + updates over time, rather than a one-off article or static profile. Directly reused as the concrete mechanism for INC42's own company-page fix.
- **Ranked borrowable ideas** (from the artifact's §5, priority-tagged): #1 build Arc-style living company pages powered by DataLabs data + editorial commentary (P1); #2 let DataLabs' already-sharp copy voice bleed into the main brand — proof-of-capability already exists, just isolated (P1); #3 add a zero-information pure-habit engagement feature, Morning Brew-style (P2); #4 publish a real ethics/standards page (P2); #5 label opinion/analysis as its own content type (P3).
