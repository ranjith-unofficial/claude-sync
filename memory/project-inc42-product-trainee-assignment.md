Ranjith is using a two-part assignment as an interview stage for [[project-inc42-product-trainee]] hiring (built 2026-08-23/24).

**Structure:**
- **Part A — take-home, ~3 hrs, 2-page cap.** Diagnose why Brief completion is low, using a rounded data pack (45% advance past card 1, ~78s median completion, ~6% return after full article, walkthrough sends new users to Explore not Brief, push never fired). Candidate must rank interventions, pick one for week 1, name a falsifiable metric, and remove one thing.
- **Part B — live in the interview, 15 min, no prep.** Visitor→signup funnel problem; real numbers handed over on the call. Deliberately live (not take-home) as an anti-AI-output safeguard — checks whether the reasoning behind Part A was actually theirs.
- Both graded on the same 4-dimension rubric shared openly with candidates: diagnosis, prioritisation, measurement, restraint (removal) — 30/30/20/20 weight.
- Includes an explicit "How we approach a problem statement" method section (6 steps) with a worked example on an unrelated product (food delivery reorder), so it teaches the reasoning shape without leaking the answer to either assignment.
- AI use is explicitly allowed for candidates; the stated countermeasure is that they must defend every claim live without notes, not that AI is banned.
